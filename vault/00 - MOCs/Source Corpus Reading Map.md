## Source discipline
The curriculum explicitly says its synthesis does not replace original documents. Return to the relevant source before high-impact implementation. Record source edition, section, status (plan versus observed deployment), contradicting later canon and unresolved facts.

The vault districts are navigation areas. Do not confuse their 13 districts with the physical Mega Campus's six canonical districts. Do not count chartered or Pending Until Activation divisions as operating teams.
