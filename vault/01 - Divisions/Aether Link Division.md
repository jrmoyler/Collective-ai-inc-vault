## Executive charter
Aether Link is a connectivity and communications technology company spanning mesh networks, language systems, verification, and resilient communications.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Improve how humans, devices, robots, organizations, and communities communicate across distance, language, and infrastructure constraints. |
| Primary industry | Telecommunications, mesh networks, language technology, translation, and global connectivity |
| Silo boundary | Owns connectivity, transmission, communication, language mediation, and presence. It does not own media content, general cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, or identity. |
| Division Director | SPECTRUM → [[Director_Aether_Link]] |
| Launch cohort | Year 3 |
