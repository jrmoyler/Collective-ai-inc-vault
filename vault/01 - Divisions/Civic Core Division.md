## Executive charter
Civic Core is Collective AI Inc.’s protected public-interest and nonprofit division, delivering digital-equity and community technology programs.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Improve public access, community infrastructure, government services, and equitable participation through technology. |
| Primary industry | Civic technology, digital equity, community systems, public-interest innovation, and municipal services |
| Silo boundary | Owns public-interest, community, digital-equity, and nonprofit outcomes. It is not a commercial marketing vehicle and does not absorb real estate, healthcare, legal, or consulting functions. |

### Source section 1
```text
3.0 Programs & Initiatives
Civic Core will execute its mission through three core, synergistic programs, each designed to leverage the unique capabilities of the Collective AI ecosystem.
3.1 The Digital Equity Initiative This program is focused on providing direct access to technology and foundational skills. We will partner with community centers in underserved neighborhoods to build and staff "Tech Hubs." These hubs will offer free access to computers, high-speed internet, and a curated set of courses from the Hybrid Living Academy.
3.2 The Community Creators Program This program is focused on fostering economic empowerment and entrepreneurship. We will run a free, cohort-based bootcamp for aspiring entrepreneurs from disadvantaged backgrounds, providing them with a free premium license to the "Creator Nexus" platform from Nexus Labs.
3.3 The Non-Profit AI Incubator This program is focused on amplifying the impact of the entire non-profit sector. We will select a cohort of local non-profits each year and act as their pro-bono AI consultant, leveraging the ZenFlow Marketplace to build and deploy custom AI agents and automations.
4.0 Community Focus & Needs Assessment
4.1 Target Community For its initial 3-5 years of operation, Civic Core will adopt a hyper-local, place-based strategy focused exclusively on underserved neighborhoods within Columbus, Ohio. This approach will allow us to build deep, authentic relationships with community leaders and develop a high-impact model that can later be replicated.
4.2 Needs Assessment Our program design is a direct response to a needs assessment of our target communities, identifying three critical gaps:
* The Digital Literacy Gap
* Barriers to Entrepreneurship
* Resource Strain on Community Organizations
5.0 Impact Measurement
Our success will be measured by a clear set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) tied directly to our programs.
* For the Digital Equity Initiative: Number of residents completing courses; weekly usage hours of Tech Hubs.
* For the Community Creators Program: Number of new businesses launched; percentage of businesses surviving after one year.
* For the Non-Profit AI Incubator: Number of non-profits served; estimated administrative hours saved by partners.
6.0 Funding & Sustainability Model
Civic Core operates on a unique and highly sustainable funding model. The organization will be launched in Year 3 of the parent company's roadmap, capitalized and sustained by a dedicated, annual allocation of profits from the for-profit ventures within the Collective AI portfolio. This model allows Civic Core to focus exclusively on its mission and long-term impact without the constant pressure of traditional fundraising.
7.0 Leadership
Civic Core will be governed by a dedicated non-profit board. Initial leadership and strategic oversight will be provided by the founders of Collective AI to ensure deep mission alignment and seamless integration of the ecosystem's resources. A dedicated Executive Director will be hired in Year 3 to lead day-to-day operations.
```
