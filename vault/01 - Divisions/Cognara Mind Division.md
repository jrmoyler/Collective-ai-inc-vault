## Executive charter
Cognara Mind is a behavioral-intelligence company applying cognitive science to products, organizations, and human-AI systems.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Understand and improve human decision-making, behavior, motivation, cognition, trust, and psychological performance. |
| Primary industry | Behavioral science, psychology, cognitive systems, decision intelligence, and mental-wellness technology |
| Silo boundary | Owns behavior, cognition, motivation, trust, and decision science. Clinical treatment remains with Vital Helix; curriculum with Hybrid Living; workforce administration is not its primary domain. |
| Division Director | MIRROR → [[Director_Cognara_Mind]] |
| Launch cohort | Year 2 |
