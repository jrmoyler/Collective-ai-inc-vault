# District Handbook — Division council

## District purpose
Division charters define capability and boundaries. Charter existence does not establish operating status. Pending divisions are outside the current twenty-division structure until activated.

Folder: 01 - Divisions. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Nine operating divisions are ZenFlow, The Collective, Hybrid Living, Nexus Labs, Signal Velocity, Quantum Ledger, Binary Loom, Obsidian Arc, and Juris Guard.
- The remaining eleven of divisions 01–20 are chartered. Divisions 21–30 are Pending Until Activation.
- Use the Division Activation Scorecard, current director registry, dependencies, and evidence when assessing readiness.

## Required record fields
- Current operating status
- Mandate and product links
- Director and human responsibility from current records
- Dependencies and activation evidence

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
