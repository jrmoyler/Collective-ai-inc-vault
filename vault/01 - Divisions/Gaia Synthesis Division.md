## Executive charter
Gaia Synthesis is a climate and agricultural intelligence company delivering environmental monitoring, resource optimization, and sustainable production systems.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Improve how agriculture, ecosystems, natural resources, emissions, and environmental risks are measured and managed. |
| Primary industry | Climate technology, agriculture, environmental intelligence, sustainability, and ecological systems |
| Silo boundary | Owns environmental, ecological, climate, and agricultural production systems. It does not own food processing, dedicated energy utilities, water utilities, or property development. |

| Launch cohort | Year 4 |
