## Executive charter
Hybrid Living is an education technology company specializing in AI literacy, adaptive learning, workforce development, and creator education.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Prepare people, schools, families, and organizations to learn, work, and thrive alongside artificial intelligence. |
| Primary industry | Education technology, workforce learning, human-AI collaboration, curriculum systems, and personal development |
| Silo boundary | Owns learning, curriculum, credential preparation, and learner outcomes. It does not own employment administration, clinical care, media distribution, or general behavioral research. |
