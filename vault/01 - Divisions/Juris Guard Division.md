3.0 Products & Services


### 3.1 JurisIQ: AI Governance Platform


The enterprise command center for AI compliance. JurisIQ provides organizations with a continuous, auditable view of their AI system risk across every model they deploy or procure.


Core capabilities:
- Model audit trail generation (decision logs, training data provenance, output monitoring)
- Bias detection reporting with remediation recommendations
- EU AI Act compliance mapping (risk classification, conformity assessment support)
- US Executive Order 14110 alignment checklist
- Third-party AI vendor risk scoring (automated questionnaire + API-based model behavior analysis)
- Automated governance reporting for board-level and regulatory submission


### 3.2 ContractForge: Contract Automation Engine


ContractForge eliminates the manual risk of contract review at scale. It applies NLP models to analyze clause-level risk, flag problematic language, and automate redlining across standard contract types.


Core capabilities:
- NLP clause risk scoring across 40+ risk categories (indemnification, IP ownership, liability caps, data privacy, AI-specific clauses)
- Automated redline generation based on client's pre-defined legal standards
- Multi-jurisdiction legal standards library (US, EU, UK, Canada baseline)
- Integration layer for Salesforce, DocuSign, and HubSpot
- Full audit trail for every contract version


### 3.3 RegPulse: Regulatory Intelligence Feed


RegPulse is the monitoring system that ensures no client is blindsided by a regulatory change. It ingests, parses, and routes regulatory developments in real time.


Core capabilities:
- Live feed ingestion from: SEC, FTC, EU AI Office, NIST, FCA, and 30+ state-level regulatory bodies
- ZenFlow-powered AI parsing that converts dense regulatory language into plain-English impact summaries
- Division-specific alert routing — each business unit only receives the regulations relevant to their operations
- Custom compliance calendar generation per client profile
- Historical regulation archive with change-delta tracking


### 3.4 Sentinel Shield: Enterprise Compliance Suite


Sentinel Shield is the full-stack compliance infrastructure for organizations operating AI at enterprise scale. It consolidates policy management, employee governance, vendor risk, and board reporting into one platform.


Core capabilities:
- AI use policy management (creation, distribution, acknowledgment tracking, version control)
- Employee AI governance training modules with completion auditing
- Vendor AI risk assessment (third-party AI procurement scoring)
- Incident response playbook automation (triggered by JurisIQ risk flags)
- Board-level compliance dashboard with regulatory exposure scoring

## 4.0 Market Analysis


### Target Market


- Enterprise Technology Companies: Organizations deploying AI at scale with board-level accountability for governance and regulatory exposure.
- Financial Services: Banks, asset managers, and FinTech firms facing SEC, FINRA, and EU AI Act requirements on algorithmic decision-making.
- Healthcare Organizations: HIPAA-adjacent AI deployments requiring auditability and FDA software guidance compliance.
- Legal Departments at Fortune 500: In-house legal teams using ContractForge to manage contract volume without proportionally scaling headcount.
- Government Contractors: Organizations subject to federal AI governance requirements under EO 14110 and NIST AI RMF.


### Competitive Landscape


- Point Solution Legal Tech (LexisNexis, Ironclad, Kira): These tools address contract review or legal research in isolation. None combine AI governance with regulatory intelligence and contract automation in a unified platform.
- Big Four Consulting on AI Governance: McKinsey, Deloitte, and PwC offer advisory services — at $500K+ engagements. Juris Guard delivers the same intelligence as a continuous SaaS subscription at a fraction of the cost.
- Compliance Point Solutions (OneTrust, TrustArc): Privacy and data compliance focused. Not built for AI-specific governance or the expanding AI regulatory landscape.


### Competitive Advantage


- AI Governance Native: Built from the inside — Juris Guard's frameworks come from actually operating 600+ AI agents across 20 divisions, not from consulting theory.
- Real-Time Regulatory Coverage: RegPulse covers the AI regulatory landscape specifically, not just general compliance — a gap no current platform fills adequately.
- Portfolio Proof: Every compliance framework Juris Guard deploys externally is tested against the Collective AI portfolio first.

## 5.0 Go-to-Market & Monetization Strategy


### Phase 1 — Internal Validation (Year 2, Months 1–6)


Deploy Juris Guard infrastructure across all 20 Collective AI divisions. Build the case study foundation, refine the product against live regulatory requirements, and establish the compliance track record that enterprise clients require before signing.


### Phase 2 — Enterprise Pilot (Year 2, Months 7–12)


Target 10 enterprise pilot clients through The Collective's existing consulting relationships. Prioritize FinTech, HealthTech, and AI-first companies where EU AI Act exposure is most immediate and the cost of non-compliance is highest.


### Phase 3 — Scale (Year 3+)


Launch the full Sentinel Shield suite to the market with documented compliance case studies. Establish channel partnerships with legal tech integrators and Big Four consulting firms who will white-label RegPulse and JurisIQ components.


### Pricing Model


- Essential: RegPulse feed + JurisIQ governance dashboard — $2,500/month
- Professional: Full JurisIQ + ContractForge + dedicated compliance agent — $8,500/month
- Enterprise: Sentinel Shield full suite + custom regulatory mapping + quarterly board reports — custom pricing
