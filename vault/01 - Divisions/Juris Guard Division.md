## Executive charter
Juris Guard is a legal technology and governance company providing contract intelligence, compliance operations, regulatory monitoring, and AI governance.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Help organizations understand, document, manage, and comply with legal, contractual, policy, and regulatory obligations. |
| Primary industry | LegalTech, regulatory intelligence, contracts, compliance, AI governance, and policy systems |
| Silo boundary | Owns legal, regulatory, policy, contract, and compliance systems. It does not provide legal representation where a licensed attorney is required, nor does it own cybersecurity, finance, or identity infrastructure. |
| Division Director | LEX → [[Director_Juris_Guard]] |
| Launch cohort | Year 2 |
