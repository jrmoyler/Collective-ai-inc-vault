## Executive charter
Nomad Nexus is a global mobility and travel-technology company connecting housing, relocation, destination intelligence, community, and remote-work operations.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Help people and organizations live, work, relocate, and operate across geographic boundaries. |
| Primary industry | Travel technology, global housing, remote-work infrastructure, relocation, and digital-nomad services |
| Silo boundary | Owns cross-border living, relocation, travel, remote-work infrastructure, housing matching, and destination experience. Terra Axis owns property systems; VectorShift owns logistics; Aether Link owns connectivity. |
| Division Director | WAYFARER → [[Director_Nomad_Nexus]] |
| Launch cohort | Year 3 |
