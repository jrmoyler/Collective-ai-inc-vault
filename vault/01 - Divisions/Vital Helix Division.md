## Executive charter
Vital Helix is a health technology company building clinically governed bio-digital intelligence and patient-support systems.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mission | Improve prevention, monitoring, diagnosis support, care coordination, and personalized health through data and governed AI. |
| Primary industry | HealthTech, digital health, biotechnology, biomedical intelligence, and bio-digital systems |
| Silo boundary | Owns clinical, biomedical, patient, and health intelligence. Nonclinical care, sports performance, general education, and longevity research remain separate. |
