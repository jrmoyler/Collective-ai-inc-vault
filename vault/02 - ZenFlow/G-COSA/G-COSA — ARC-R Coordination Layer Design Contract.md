# G-COSA — ARC-R Coordination Layer Design Contract

> [!info] Research architecture, not deployed behavior
> Source v1.0 defines mathematical models and software contracts. Claims about available simulators or curvature priors require verification; advanced geometry/affective sensing is a research plan. Corrected hardware allocation remains in [[G-COSA — Physical Compute Matrix]]. Current oversight follows [[Agent Tier Registry]].

## Source contract
```text
2.5 ARC-R Coordination Layer
ARC-R dynamically couples and decouples agents, divisions, and workflows based on geometric
compatibility and operational needs.
Coupling modes:
Coupled — agents move as one unit
Soft-coupled — shared briefing but independent execution
Decoupled — fully isolated to prevent interference
Quarantined — isolated due to divergence or instability
Human-gated — requires explicit review before execution
Quiet mode — suppressed communication under high operator load
```
