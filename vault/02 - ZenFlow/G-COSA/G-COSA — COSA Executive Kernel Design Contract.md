# G-COSA — COSA Executive Kernel Design Contract

> [!info] Research architecture, not deployed behavior
> Source v1.0 defines mathematical models and software contracts. Claims about available simulators or curvature priors require verification; advanced geometry/affective sensing is a research plan. Corrected hardware allocation remains in [[G-COSA — Physical Compute Matrix]]. Current oversight follows [[Agent Tier Registry]].

## Source contract
```text
2.10 COSA Executive Kernel
COSA is the operating system kernel. It does not reason. It does not optimize. It decides what runs
when.
Core responsibilities:
Sense — receive telemetry from all layers
Interpret — convert telemetry into regime, curvature, and ambiguity signals
Reason — invoke HGLAR + HRM for ambiguous states
Plan — invoke Geodesic Planner for trajectories
Coordinate — invoke ARC-R for coupling state
Optimize — invoke Natural Gradient Optimizer
Govern — apply Symbiotic AI and ERC constraints
Remember — log regime charts, reasoning traces, and outcomes
COSA system modes include: normal, clarify, compress, de-escalate, slow_down, human_review,
trust_repair, crisis_response.
```
