# G-COSA — Curvature Detection Engine Design Contract

> [!info] Research architecture, not deployed behavior
> Source v1.0 defines mathematical models and software contracts. Claims about available simulators or curvature priors require verification; advanced geometry/affective sensing is a research plan. Corrected hardware allocation remains in [[G-COSA — Physical Compute Matrix]]. Current oversight follows [[Agent Tier Registry]].

## Source contract
```text
2.2 Curvature Detection Engine
The engine continuously estimates geometric stress using Hessian traces, Fisher eigenvalues, Jacobian
spectrum analysis, and graph Laplacians.
Core outputs include:
Ricci scalar — systemic compression or expansion
Sectional curvature — localized directional instability
Fisher drift — rate of manifold geometry change
Synchronization risk — probability of agent coordination collapse
```
