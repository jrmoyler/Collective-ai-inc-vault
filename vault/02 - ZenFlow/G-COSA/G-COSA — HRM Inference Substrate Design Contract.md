> [!info] Research architecture, not deployed behavior

Corrected hardware allocation remains in [[G-COSA — Physical Compute Matrix]]. Current oversight follows [[Agent Tier Registry]].

## Source contract
```text
2.7 HRM Inference Substrate
HRM (Hierarchical Reasoning Models) is the inference engine that runs over the HGLAR graph. It
performs bidirectional reasoning across five levels:
Figure 2: HRM Five-Level Hierarchical Reasoning
Bottom-up aggregation: Beliefs propagate from agents upward through clusters, divisions, and global
state.
Top-down constraint propagation: Global context and governance rules cascade downward to
suppress local overreactions.
Cross-level conflict resolution: When agent clusters disagree with division priors, HRM resolves the
conflict using curvature, evidence weight, and human-context state.
HRM HierarchyHRM Reasoning Depth Policy
Low curvature + low ambiguity: depth = 1
High curvature + low ambiguity: depth = 2
Low curvature + high ambiguity: depth = 3
High curvature + high ambiguity + capital impact:
 depth = 4 or human review
```
