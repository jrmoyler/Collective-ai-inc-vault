```text
4. Advanced Cognitive Subsystems
4.1 HGLAR Reasoning Framework & HRM Substrate
When an instruction is ambiguous, G-COSA does not guess. It builds a graph.
* HGLAR (Hierarchical Graph Learning and Reasoning): Decomposes complex situations into sub-questions. Each node carries geometric and emotional context.
* HRM (Hierarchical Reasoning Models): Bidirectional reasoning. Bottom-up aggregation (beliefs propagate from the 30 agents up to the Division Overseer) and Top-down constraint propagation (Global context cascaded downward).
   * Depth Policy: If an issue has high ambiguity and high capital impact, HRM triggers a "depth=4" computation or halts for Human Review via the Master Terminal.
4.2 MELD/ERC Affective-Social Sensing
Using HGLER (Hierarchical Graph Learning for Emotion Recognition) concepts, the system monitors your interactions (via WhatsApp/Telegram frontends or the terminal).
* Frustration/Confusion detected: Triggers operator_stress. COSA compresses explanations and reduces options to lower your cognitive load.
* Hesitancy/Rapid interruption: Triggers trust_drop. The Overseer exposes evidence graphs and slows down execution.
4.3 ARC-R Coordination Layer
ARC-R dynamically couples agents. Instead of all 600 agents talking at once across the network:
* Coupled: Agents move as one unit.
* Soft-coupled: Shared briefing, independent execution.
* Quarantined: Isolated due to geometric divergence or instability.
* Quiet Mode: Suppresses agent-to-agent chatter when the human operator is experiencing high operator_stress.
```
