# G-COSA — Symbiotic AI Governance Design Contract

> [!info] Research architecture, not deployed behavior
> Source v1.0 defines mathematical models and software contracts. Claims about available simulators or curvature priors require verification; advanced geometry/affective sensing is a research plan. Corrected hardware allocation remains in [[G-COSA — Physical Compute Matrix]]. Current oversight follows [[Agent Tier Registry]].

## Source contract
```text
2.9 Symbiotic AI Governance
Symbiotic AI is the governance layer ensuring human-AI co-adaptation rather than unilateral automation.
It defines:
• Autonomy levels per division and per action classHuman review triggers
Explanation requirements
Consent boundaries
Audit artifact generation
Feedback ingestion loops
Autonomy levels are not global. They are scoped:
Research agents: autonomy_level = 4
Trading execution agents: autonomy_level = 1 or 2
Capital allocation agents: autonomy_level = 1
Operational routing agents: autonomy_level = 3
Governance / legal agents: autonomy_level = 1
```
