Collective AI Inc. · Ecosystem Dossier 020 1 · T H E I N V I T A T I O N Who we are, who this is for
Built as operating fabric,
not as a product catalog.
Collective AI Inc. is a venture studio running 20 departments and a 600-agent lattice on a single
governance roof. This dossier is the working map we hand to prospective partners — operators,
capital, distribution, and capability.
W H AT T H I S I S
A walk-through of the wiring.
Every department is a real capability domain with a P&L, a product
surface, and a customer motion. The cross-department market plays —
Synergy Nodes — are the products you would actually license, co-build, or
distribute.
If you read one section, read the Partnership Alignment Matrix on page 11.
That table is the shortest path from "I run X business" to "here is which
division opens the door."
W H AT W E A R E N O T
We are not a research lab, not a single-product company, and not a
marketplace. We do not acquire startups for portfolio decoration — we
staff departments, ship products, and only then look for partners who
extend distribution or capability.
T H E P R O M I S E
Three things we will not compromise.
Reversible by default. No agent executes an irreversible action without a
human in the loop. Capital allocation, governance calls, and trading
execution sit at autonomy 1–2. Research and routing run at 3–4.
Revenue funds revenue. Phase 1 revenue funds Phase 2. Phase 2 funds
Phase 3. No Phase 4 node is built before Phase 1 generates proof of
revenue. The roadmap is a cash ladder, not a wishlist.
Regulated zones stay regulated. Civic Core is 501(c)(3) and
untouchable commercially. Health and longevity flows route through
clinical oversight. Anything touching capital markets waits on legal opinion
before deployment.

COVERAGE

T H E H A R D R U L E
Phase 1 funds Phase 2. No exceptions.
The 20 Synergy Nodes are sequenced in 4 phases. Phase 1 (5 nodes) must generate proof of revenue before Phase 2 ships. Phase 2 must reach its ARR
band before Phase 3 builds out. We do not run a Phase 4 civilization play on unbacked Phase 1 promises. This is what makes the studio durable — and
what makes a partner's time on a deal feel safe.
