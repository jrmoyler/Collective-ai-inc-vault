## Full Synergy Node Catalog (40 nodes)
Summary of the [[Full Synergy Node Catalog]].
- **Digital Synergy Nodes (20, D-01 to D-20):** cross-division market execution units. Each bundles two or more divisions into one product with its own product surface, revenue model, and ZenFlow agent cluster. Four phases: 1 Revenue & Proof (D-01 to D-05), 2 Protection & Premium (D-06 to D-10), 3 IP & Lifestyle (D-11 to D-15), 4 Civilization Infrastructure (D-16 to D-20). Total ARR potential stated as $200M+ (target; $0 MRR as of Sept 2026).
- **Physical Synergy Nodes (20, SYN-01 to SYN-20):** hardware fusion devices, each serving two or more divisions. Hardware from the Foundry catalog, intelligence from the ZenFlow 600-agent stack, Aegis Protocol governance on every node. Five phases: 1 Fabrication Anchor ([[SYN-13 Binary Forge Station]] first), 2 Intelligence + Sensing (11 non-actuating nodes), 3 Mobility + Actuation (Aegis-Hold until e-stop verification), 4 Aerial + Field (Aegis-Hold until FAA authorization), 5 Command Infrastructure ([[SYN-20 Zenith Orchestration Tower]]). Total build cost ~$52K–$80K.
- **Phase funding rule:** Phase 1 funds Phase 2. Phase 2 funds Phase 3. Phase 3 funds Phase 4. Do not build Phase 4 before Phase 1 proves revenue and operating discipline. This applies to both digital and physical nodes.
- **Naming:** of the older node names in this handbook, Kinetic Scholar matches [[D-05 Kinetic Scholar]]. Conscious Grid, Behavioral Forge, and Sovereign Mind are not in the catalog; the catalog list is current.
- Helios Grid / Volt Nexus stays blocked pending SEC legal opinion. The catalog's Stanley Constant veto on [[D-17 Civic Nervous System]] was removed Oct 1, 2026 ([[Civic Core Fiduciary Veto]]).
