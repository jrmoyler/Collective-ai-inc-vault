## Strategic Logic
Public-interest digital infrastructure combining Civic Core's mission layer, Aether Link's connectivity, and ZenFlow's intelligence. Closes the digital divide by providing both the physical connectivity infrastructure and the AI intelligence layer to communities that have neither.

## Market
Municipal governments, NGOs, international development organizations, and foundations building digital equity and public-interest AI infrastructure.

## What This Node Delivers
- Civic Core digital equity programs and community access infrastructure
- Aether Link mesh connectivity for underserved communities
- ZenFlow intelligence layer providing AI access to digitally excluded populations
- Multilingual AI access via Babel AI translation
- Grant coordination and impact measurement infrastructure

## Products
- Civic Nervous System Platform — community digital equity intelligence system
- Community Mesh Network — Aether Link connectivity for underserved areas
- Civic AI Access Portal — multilingual AI services for public use

## Services
