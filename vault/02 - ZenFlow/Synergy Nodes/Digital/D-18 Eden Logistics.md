## Strategic Logic
Autonomous food logistics combined with agricultural intelligence and mesh communications. VectorShift routes the vehicles. Gaia Synthesis tracks the food. Aether Link connects every farm, warehouse, and distribution point. Together they build the intelligent food supply chain.

## Market
