## Strategic Logic
Robotics + AgriTech + AI. Fully autonomous food production systems. Animus Prime's agricultural robots operating in Gaia Synthesis's AI-managed farms, coordinated by ZenFlow's intelligence. Food production at civilizational scale without human labor dependency.

## Market
Large-scale agricultural operations, sovereign food security programs, and urban farming infrastructure investors seeking fully autonomous food production.

## What This Node Delivers
- Animus Prime agricultural robots for harvesting, planting, and monitoring
- Gaia Synthesis farm AI managing crop conditions, pest management, and yield optimization
- ZenFlow coordinating robot fleets with farm intelligence in real time
- Fully autonomous farm operation from soil preparation to harvest
- Sovereign food security infrastructure for governments and large operators
