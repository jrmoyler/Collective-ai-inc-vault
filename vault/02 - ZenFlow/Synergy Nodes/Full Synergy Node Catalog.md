# Full Synergy Node Catalog

Reference for every Synergy Node in the Collective AI ecosystem, from the 42-page *Collective AI Full Synergy Node Catalog* by John-Ross Moyler ([[JR Moyler]]). It defines 40 nodes in two parallel architectures. Digital nodes are market execution units that generate revenue. Physical nodes are hardware fusion devices that make intelligence operational. Both are governed by ZenFlow, [[Aegis Protocol]], and [[Knowledge Keeper]].

| Architecture | Count | Phases | Headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital market execution nodes | 20 (D-01 to D-20) | 4 | Total ARR potential: $200M+ (target) |
| Physical hardware fusion nodes | 20 (SYN-01 to SYN-20) | 5 | Total build cost: ~$52K–$80K |

> [!info] Targets, not results
> Every ARR figure below is a planning target. Collective AI has 0 paying customers and $0 MRR as of Sept 2026.

> [!warning] Build rule
> Phase 1 funds Phase 2. Phase 2 funds Phase 3. Phase 3 funds Phase 4. Do not build Phase 4 before Phase 1 proves revenue and operating discipline. This applies to both digital and physical nodes.

> [!danger] Blocked
> Helios Grid / Volt Nexus remains blocked pending Juris Guard SEC legal opinion on ENRG token classification. See [[Helios Grid]].

> [!note] Superseded
> The catalog says: "Civic Nervous System — all program decisions require Stanley Constant independent veto review." That veto was removed on Oct 1, 2026. See [[Civic Core Fiduciary Veto]].

### Digital phases
- **Phase 1 — Revenue & Proof:** These five nodes are the immediate build priority. They generate revenue, validate the Synergy Node architecture, and fund all subsequent phases. Nodes: [[D-01 Resonance Media]], [[D-02 Ascension Campus]], [[D-03 Quantum Commerce Grid]], [[D-04 Oracle Relay]], [[D-05 Kinetic Scholar]]
- **Phase 2 — Protection & Premium:** Protection and premium services built once Phase 1 generates sustainable cash flow. Higher price points, enterprise focus, longer sales cycles. Nodes: [[D-06 Founder Ark]], [[D-07 Signal Court]], [[D-08 Ghost Protocol]], [[D-09 Aegis Forge]], [[D-10 Blackbox Citadel]]
- **Phase 3 — IP & Lifestyle:** IP generation, lifestyle infrastructure, and real estate intelligence. Built once Phase 2's protection layer is generating enterprise ARR. Nodes: [[D-11 Mythos]], [[D-12 Nomad Market]], [[D-13 BioSovereign]], [[D-14 TerraMind]], [[D-15 Sovereign Assets]]
- **Phase 4 — Civilization Infrastructure:** Civilization-scale infrastructure. These nodes are the long-horizon vision — what Collective AI becomes when Phases 1–3 have generated the capital, proof, and relationships to operate at this scale. Nodes: [[D-16 Civilization Twin]], [[D-17 Civic Nervous System]], [[D-18 Eden Logistics]], [[D-19 Mecha Orchard]], [[D-20 Momentum Voyages]]

### Physical phases
- **Phase 1 — Fabrication Anchor:** Build the Binary Forge Station first — it produces custom enclosures and parts for every subsequent physical node, reducing per-node cost throughout the entire build sequence. Nodes: [[SYN-13 Binary Forge Station]]
- **Phase 2 — Intelligence + Sensing:** Wearables, terminals, compliance rigs, and intelligence kits. The human-scale sensing and data intelligence layer. 11 nodes, no actuation — these can all be built and operated before Phase 3 Aegis-Hold clearance. Nodes: [[SYN-01 Sentinel Guardian]], [[SYN-02 Apex Recovery Station]], [[SYN-05 Resonance Compliance Rig]], [[SYN-07 Aurum Biosignal Band]], [[SYN-09 Cognara Consulting Kit]], [[SYN-11 Nexus Signal Broadcast Node]], [[SYN-12 Nomad Intelligence Kit]], [[SYN-14 Terra Habitat Sentinel]], [[SYN-15 Signal Cohort Badge]], [[SYN-18 Quantum Signal Terminal]], [[SYN-19 Vital Neuro Wristband]]
- **Phase 3 — Mobility + Actuation:** Kiosks, rovers, and labs. Ground-level mobility and human interaction surfaces. Aegis-Hold for all actuation nodes until e-stop verification is complete. Nodes: [[SYN-04 Herald Campus Kiosk]], [[SYN-08 Prime Shepherd Rover]], [[SYN-10 Gaia Field Rover]], [[SYN-16 Civic Babel Kiosk]], [[SYN-17 Eon Performance Lab]]
- **Phase 4 — Aerial + Field:** Survey drone and relay drone. Aerial intelligence and mesh extension. Aegis-Hold until FAA authorization is confirmed for the operational area. Nodes: [[SYN-03 Terra Gaia Survey Drone]], [[SYN-06 Aether Relay Drone]]
- **Phase 5 — Command Infrastructure:** The full Foundry command layer — ZENITH Overseer, Knowledge Keeper Vault, 30-node cluster, 600-agent lattice. Build this last; it becomes the most powerful when everything else is already connected. Nodes: [[SYN-20 Zenith Orchestration Tower]]

## Shared Elements
- Physical node hardware comes from the Foundry catalog; intelligence comes from the ZenFlow 600-agent stack. Every node runs on Aegis Protocol governance.
- [[Knowledge Keeper]] appears in every physical node's agent list.
- Physical events and software agent flags share one Aegis queue (see [[SYN-01 Sentinel Guardian]] and [[SYN-20 Zenith Orchestration Tower]]).
- The catalog gives one budget per physical node, not per-part prices, and does not list build steps or named agent clusters for digital nodes.

## Naming Differences From Older Vault Text
Older vault text named four nodes: Kinetic Scholar, Conscious Grid, Behavioral Forge, and Sovereign Mind.
- **Kinetic Scholar** matches this catalog: [[D-05 Kinetic Scholar]].
- **Conscious Grid**, **Behavioral Forge**, and **Sovereign Mind** do not appear in this catalog. The closest names are [[D-15 Sovereign Assets]] (Quantum Ledger + Terra Axis + Juris Guard) and Cognara Mind's physical nodes ([[SYN-09 Cognara Consulting Kit]], [[SYN-19 Vital Neuro Wristband]]). Treat the catalog list as current.
