## Why These Divisions Fuse
Quantum Ledger tracks market signals and financial stress events. Vital Helix tracks biological stress responses. The intersection is decision quality — a trader's physiological state at the moment of a high-stakes trade directly affects outcome. This wearable cross-references financial signal state with biometric stress indicators.

## Product Description
A dual-function wearable receiving market signal haptic alerts from the Quantum Ledger Aurum Terminal while monitoring the wearer's biometric stress state. The Biosignal Fusion Task Agent correlates the financial signal state with the wearer's biological readiness score. If a high-stakes trade fires while biometrics indicate elevated stress, the wristband delivers a "caution" vibration pattern. Trade execution confirmation requires a deliberate double-press, not a reflex response.
