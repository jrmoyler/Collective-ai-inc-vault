## Why These Divisions Fuse
The Collective runs high-stakes consulting engagements where client decision quality and trust calibration directly affect outcomes. Cognara Mind studies human-AI trust and cognitive load. The consulting session is the natural research environment — with consent, Cognara Mind instruments the room where The Collective delivers strategic work.

## Product Description
A portable consulting session intelligence kit equipping The Collective client sessions with Cognara Mind behavioral data collection. The kit deploys unobtrusively — consent-first ambient sensing captures non-verbal engagement signals while the Consulting Director Agent runs the session workflow. The Cognara Behavioral Task Agent generates session engagement metrics without ever recording identifiable biometric data.

## Hardware Components (Bill of Materials)
The catalog gives one estimated budget for the whole build (~$320–$480); it does not price parts individually.
- Raspberry Pi 5 8GB — dual-pipeline session controller
- ReSpeaker 4-Mic Array v2.0 — voice prosody + engagement capture
- Pi Camera Module 3 — non-identifying engagement presence sensing
- Whisplay HAT — consent status display + session mode indicator
- Physical Consent Button — one-press opt-in for behavioral layer
- Pi M.2 HAT+ + 1TB NVMe (encrypted) — dual partition: consulting + research
- Portable LiPo battery + 65W GaN charger
