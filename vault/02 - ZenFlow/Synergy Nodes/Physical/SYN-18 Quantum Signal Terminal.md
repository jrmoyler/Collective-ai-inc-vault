## Why These Divisions Fuse
Quantum Ledger tracks financial performance. Signal Velocity tracks growth performance. Both are performance dashboards. A market volatility event that impacts the portfolio may also be the right moment to pause paid acquisition; a Signal Velocity conversion spike may indicate the right moment to scale ad spend. This terminal enables cross-signal decision intelligence that neither division has alone.

## Product Description
A dual-display Pi terminal showing Quantum Ledger's live portfolio and trade signal dashboard on one Whisplay panel and Signal Velocity's funnel metrics and campaign performance on the second. A Cross-Signal Intelligence Task Agent continuously monitors both data streams for correlated events and delivers unified alerts to the founder's Zenith Command Band via haptic pattern.
