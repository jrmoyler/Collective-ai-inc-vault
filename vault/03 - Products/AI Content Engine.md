## MVP plan

MVP brief: [[MVP — AI Content Engine]] (from [[MVP Build Guide]])

- Objective: ZenFlow-powered content production system turning raw ideas into posts, briefings, emails, and campaigns.
- Build platform: Claude API, n8n automation, Notion calendar, Buffer/Later, Klaviyo/Beehiiv.
- Priority: Phase 1/2; complexity: Low-Medium
- Governance: Standard Aegis-Clear with privacy, audit logging, and role-based access controls.
- Timeline: 90 days in 5 phases; status planned.
