## MVP plan

MVP brief: [[MVP — AI Workforce Transformation Program]] (from [[MVP Build Guide]])

- Objective: Multi-week enterprise upskilling program for prompt engineering, AI workflows, collaboration, and role-specific adoption.
- Build platform: Hybrid Living Atlas, Zoom/in-person training, LMS progress tracking, Notion/Google Docs.
- Priority: Phase 1/2; complexity: Low-Medium

- Timeline: 90 days in 5 phases; status planned.
