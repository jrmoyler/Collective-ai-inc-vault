## Source-defined product
AI Performance Coach Our B2C subscription platform for serious amateur and professional athletes. The Apex System integrates with wearable data and user input to act as a 24/7 AI coach, providing dynamic, personalized plans for training, nutrition, recovery, and mental preparedness, all while using predictive analytics to mitigate injury risk.
