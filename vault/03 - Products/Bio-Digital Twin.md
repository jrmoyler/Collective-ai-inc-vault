Longitudinal health model integrating biomarkers, wearables, labs, and history. Reframed for physicians as a chart twin for pre-charting and reconciliation.

## MVP plan

MVP brief: [[MVP — Bio-Digital Twin]] (from [[MVP Build Guide]])

- Objective: AI-powered biological state replica integrating biomarkers, wearables, genomics, and health history to simulate trajectories.
- Build platform: Next.js/React, FastAPI, ZenFlow/Claude, Apple Health, Oura, Whoop, LabCorp/Quest, 23andMe.
- Priority: Year 4 - Series C; complexity: High
- Governance: Aegis-Review: clinical oversight, HIPAA/privacy, no unsupported medical claims.
- Timeline: 90 days in 5 phases; status planned.

## Master Product Catalog entry

- **Division:** [[Vital Helix Division]]
- **Type:** Health Intelligence Platform

**Description.** AI-powered digital replica of a user's biological state. Integrates biomarker data, wearable sensor streams, genomic data, and health history to simulate health trajectories and optimize interventions. Powered by Zenith OS.

**Build / creation platform.** Next.js / React frontend. FastAPI backend. ZenFlow / Claude API for simulation. Wearable integration: Apple Health, Oura, Whoop APIs. Lab data via LabCorp / Quest API. Genomics via 23andMe API.

> [!warning] Clinical oversight
> Per the catalog, all health and longevity recommendations from Vital Helix and Eon Core require clinical oversight before delivery. Aegis-Review is mandatory. See [[Aegis Protocol]].
