# Content Distribution Engine

**Division:** [[Signal Velocity Division]] · **Type:** Paid Content Distribution · **Status:** spec

## Description

Paid content distribution infrastructure for Nexus Labs content and division marketing. Boosts organic content, manages influencer campaigns, and optimizes distribution for audience quality.

## Build / creation platform

Meta / LinkedIn Ads APIs for content promotion. Influencer management via Grin or AspireIQ. Attribution tracking via Northbeam. n8n for distribution automation.
