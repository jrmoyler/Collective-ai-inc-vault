# District Handbook — Product workshop

## District purpose
A product note connects a user problem to a specification and evidence of delivery. A catalog entry does not mean a product is shipped or earning revenue.

Folder: 03 - Products. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Separate specification, prototype, deployed service, and paid adoption evidence.
- Link technical dependencies, acceptance criteria, task records, and the owning division.
- Helios Grid remains blocked pending SEC legal opinion. Preserve the hold and source record; do not turn its roadmap into a current offering.

## Required record fields
- Problem and intended user
- Observed product state
- Acceptance criteria and dependency links
- Delivery evidence and known limitations

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
