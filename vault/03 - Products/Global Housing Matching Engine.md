- Build platform: Python recommendations, Selina/Outpost APIs, PostgreSQL listings, Mapbox, Stripe.

**Description.** AI-powered matching engine connecting nomads with co-living spaces, serviced apartments, and direct rentals globally. Matches on budget, amenities, community density, connectivity, and duration.
