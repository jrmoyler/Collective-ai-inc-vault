## Master Product Catalog entry

Catalog name: **Longevity Research Intelligence Platform**
- **Division:** [[Eon Core Division]]
- **Type:** Research Monitoring Platform

**Description.** Continuous monitoring and synthesis of longevity science publications. Tracks journals (Nature Aging, Cell, Aging Cell, Geroscience), labs (Sinclair, Altos, Calico), and clinical trials. Weekly intelligence for community and platform.

**Build / creation platform.** PubMed API + bioRxiv monitor. ClinicalTrials.gov API. Claude API for research synthesis. n8n for automated weekly brief generation. PostgreSQL for research library.
