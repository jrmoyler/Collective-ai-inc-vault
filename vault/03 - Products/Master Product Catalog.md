# Master Product Catalog

The Collective AI Master Product, Service & Platform Catalog (45 pages) lists every product, service and tool across the 20 chartered divisions, with the exact build platform for each product. Tagline: "Architecting a Humane Future." The catalog describes the portfolio as ZenFlow-powered with a 600-agent lattice.
