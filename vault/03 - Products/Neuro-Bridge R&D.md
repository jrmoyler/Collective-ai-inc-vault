## Source-defined product
A revolutionary assistive interface for non-verbal individuals. Using non-invasive gaze tracking and predictive AI, it translates micro-movements and intent into fluent, synthesized speech, allowing those with conditions like ALS or severe autism to communicate at the speed of thought.
