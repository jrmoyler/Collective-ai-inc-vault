## MVP plan

MVP brief: [[MVP — Nomad Nexus Platform]] (from [[MVP Build Guide]])

- Objective: All-in-one platform for visa intelligence, housing, destination analytics, cost of living, connectivity, and community.
- Build platform: Next.js/React Native, Supabase, PostgreSQL visa data, matching algorithm, Mapbox, Stripe.
- Priority: 12 (build order #12); complexity: Medium
- Governance: Standard Aegis-Clear with privacy, audit logging, and role-based access controls.
- Timeline: 90 days in 5 phases; status planned.
