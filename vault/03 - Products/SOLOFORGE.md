# SOLOFORGE

AI-native corporation foundry for vertically integrated Solo Corporations. Turns one person into a solo corporation. First action from the Disney three-room session: a one-page offer with a name and price.

## Offer

**SOLOFORGE**

The AI-native corporation foundry for the solo corporation.

SOLOFORGE turns one person into a complete corporation. It builds the entity, the operating system, and the AI agent workforce around a single owner. A solo founder gets the structure and output of a full company without hiring one.

**SOLOFORGE Launch — $2,500 one-time**
Ohio LLC filed with the state fee covered, EIN secured, single-member operating agreement, statutory agent for year one, business bank account guidance, brand basics with a one-page site, and one AI agent workflow built and tested. Includes an in-person working session in Columbus and 30 days of support.

**SOLOFORGE Complete — $5,000 one-time**
Everything in Launch, plus the full operating system: SOPs, playbooks, and CRM setup. Up to three AI agent workflows. Ninety days of support.

**Care plan — $500/month, optional**
Monitoring, a monthly review, ongoing optimization, and priority builds.

**Built for:** founders and independent operators who work alone and want to operate at company scale.

**How the price was set:** Collective AI Universal Pricing Framework v1.0. Comparable market bundles (formation services, business-in-a-box offers, AI setup agencies) run $5,000 to $10,000 for this outcome. DIY runs about $2,000 in hard costs plus the founder's build time. Sweet spot lands at $2,500 for Launch and $5,000 for Complete. Entry tier, productized, standard complexity, CAP held at 1.0 for launch.
