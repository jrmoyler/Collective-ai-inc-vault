## Master Product Catalog entry

- **Division:** [[Vital Helix Division]]

**Description.** Video and async telehealth infrastructure connecting Vital Helix users with licensed physicians for Bio-Digital Twin interpretation, Custom Script co-signing, and personalized health consultations.
