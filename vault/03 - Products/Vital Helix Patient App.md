## Master Product Catalog entry

- **Division:** [[Vital Helix Division]]
- **Type:** Consumer Health App

**Description.** Consumer-facing health intelligence app. Integrates Bio-Digital Twin data, Neuro-Pulse protocols, lab results, wearable data, and personalized health coaching into a unified daily health interface.

**Build / creation platform.** React Native. Supabase HIPAA-compliant backend. Apple Health / Google Fit integration. Push notifications via Expo. Stripe for subscription billing.

> [!warning] Clinical oversight
> Per the catalog, all health and longevity recommendations from Vital Helix and Eon Core require clinical oversight before delivery. Aegis-Review is mandatory. See [[Aegis Protocol]].
