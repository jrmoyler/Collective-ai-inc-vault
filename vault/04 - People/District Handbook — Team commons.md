# District Handbook — Team commons

## District purpose
People notes describe responsibilities and collaboration context from current records. They must not invent titles, ownership, or sensitive personal details.

Folder: 04 - People. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Required record fields
- Current recorded name and role
- Division and project responsibilities
- Responsibility source
- Open coordination question

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
