# Senior Advisor Observation Memo

## Assessment record
| Area | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|
| Market | Customer problem, actual demand and competitive alternatives |
| Product | Working release versus demo, roadmap assumptions and unknowns |
| Economics | Actual burn, unit costs, runway inputs and pricing version |
| Capital | Real funding need, terms, founder-control risks and target fit |
| Governance | Audit path, data lineage, regulatory review and shutdown controls |
| Leadership | Decision alignment, unresolved tradeoffs and ownership |

## Memo structure
1. Scope and date of reviewed material.
2. Observed strengths, weaknesses and blind spots.
3. Critical assumptions with evidence/uncertainty.
4. Strategic options and the constraint that decides among them.
5. Proposed action, CEO decision needed and follow-up evidence.

## First-quarter output trail
Strategic Observation Memo → narrative/market refinement → authorized meetings and quarterly review. Attach actual review dates and source revisions; leave completion blank until observed.

The packet suggests capital/partnership-linked compensation and possible future role evolution. Those proposals are not executed terms. Never describe targeted capital, success fees or prospective board access as secured. Link [[Arthur Fayne]], [[Financial Position]] and [[Decision Record Template]].
