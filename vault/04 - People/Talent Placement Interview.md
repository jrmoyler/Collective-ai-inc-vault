# Talent Placement Interview

The source questionnaire seeks fit through examples and motivations; it is not a hiring decision or automatic ranking system.

## Information to collect
Name / authorized contact / portfolio or resume / actual experience / preferred division / motivation.

## Problem-solving discussion
Ask how the person first approaches an unfamiliar problem: research, experiment, seek experienced guidance or map the whole system. Request a real example to avoid treating one multiple-choice answer as proof of ability.

## Interest signals
The source contrasts strategy, automated operations, creative media, sports prediction and community education scenarios. It then asks 1–5 self-ratings for teaching, automation, data analysis, strategy-to-execution, social impact, storytelling and performance analysis.

## Placement record
Evidence / interests / proposed track / role requirements / artifacts to review / interviewer / remaining questions / actual agreed next step.

The historical form lists seven departments and sports/viral scale examples. Those are prompt scenarios, not current company scale or completed work. Match placement against the current 20-division canon, nine operating divisions and [[Open Seats]], not the old dropdown alone.

Use [[Team Learning Path and Competency Gates]] for training and [[SOP — Team Onboarding]] only after a real role/access decision.
