# Client AI Readiness Interview

Use the actual questionnaire's three sections for discovery. Collect answers in the authorized client record, not in a shared public note.

## Vision
- Name, company and role.
- Business mission and desired change for customers/industry.
- What success would look like over the next 12–18 months.

## Operations
- Technology confidence on the source's 1–5 scale: starting out, basic use, effective current tools, active adoption or technology-led practice.
- One repetitive task the team most wants to remove.
- Up to three priority areas: customer acquisition, content/marketing, data decisions, operations, training, finances, or a named other area.

## AI starting point
- Initial response to AI: excitement, curiosity, uncertainty, skepticism or concern about cost/complexity.
- The most important AI question to answer first.

## Discovery output
Translate the responses into one bounded problem, affected users, existing tools/data, desired outcome and unanswered facts. Use [[Pricing Proposal Worksheet]] to capture budget separately; the readiness questionnaire itself does not establish affordability, technical feasibility or permission to access data.

The source promises contact within one business day. Treat that as a proposed service standard that requires an actual assigned owner; do not assert an interview was reviewed or a call booked.
