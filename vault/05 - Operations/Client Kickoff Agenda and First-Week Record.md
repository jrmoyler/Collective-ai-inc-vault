# Client Kickoff Agenda and First-Week Record

The client-onboarding source divides work into the 48-hour handoff, 60-minute kickoff and first seven days. Apply current contract/payment rules from [[SOP — Client Onboarding]].

## Handoff
Assign one project lead. Prepare approved scope/terms and invoice. Brief the lead on the client problem, goal and sales history. Send a welcome and booking path only after signed agreement and required payment. Track who owns each action; source text is not authorization to email or invite anyone.

## Kickoff agenda
| Minutes | Topic | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Participants and roles | Project lead, specialists, client contact and approver |
| 15 | Problem and desired outcome | Client's own definition of success |
| 15 | Success measures | Three to five agreed, measurable indicators |
| 10 | Scope and timeline | Contract/version, milestones and exclusions |
| 10 | Communication/logistics | Agreed channel, weekly cadence and approval contacts |
| 5 | Immediate next steps | Named owners and actual due dates |

The source's percentage and training-count KPI examples are illustrations, not promised outcomes.

## First-week evidence
- [ ] Within the source's proposed three-hour window, prepare a kickoff summary with decisions and action owners.
- [ ] Establish the mutually approved communication space.
- [ ] Schedule actual agreed recurring reviews.
- [ ] Deliver the first tangible contracted artifact, such as a data request, audit or research summary.
- [ ] Log acknowledgment, blockers and next milestone.

Use [[Project Delivery Record Template]] to store acceptance and [[Client Billing Ledger]] for payment state.
