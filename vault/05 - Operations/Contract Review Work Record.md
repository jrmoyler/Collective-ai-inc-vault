# Contract Review Work Record

> [!note] Internal review workflow
> This records the company's source playbook and does not state what the law permits, that a clause is enforceable, or that a contract is approved. Applicable law and executed terms require review of the actual agreement.

## Clause review matrix
| Area | Record for the actual agreement |
|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Protected material, permitted disclosure, duration and return/destruction |
| IP | Pre-existing assets, work product, licenses, sublicensing and assignment |
| Liability | Caps, exclusions, carveouts, indemnity and defense control |
| Term/termination | Renewal, exit rights, cure, notice and transition obligations |
| Payment | Amounts, dates, contingent events, expenses and accounting rights |
| Restrictions | Competition/solicitation scope, consideration and jurisdiction review |
| Warranties | Scope, qualifiers, uncertain outcomes and third-party materials |
| Disputes | Forum, escalation, arbitration/litigation and equitable relief |
| Employment | Actual role, status, duties, benefits and IP assignment |
| Collaboration | Defined project, resources, milestones, independence and wind-down |

## Source escalation categories
A: core-IP assignment, uncapped exposure, personal guarantees, business-blocking exclusivity or comparable existential restrictions.
B: severe one-sided risk, uncontrolled compliance promises, third-party rights guarantees, retroactive exposure or outcome guarantees.
C: operational issues such as renewal/exit, assignment, excessive exclusivity or minimum purchase obligations.

Category A requires immediate legal escalation in the source. Record the clause, impact, requested edit, reviewer and decision; do not substitute a generated risk label for legal approval.

## Before signature
Complete party names, addresses, dates, payment, IP, confidentiality, exit and liability terms. Record resolved flags, actual signature authority and legal review where required by the source. This note executes no agreement.

The source contains both fixed-term and at-will employment language and several jurisdiction-sensitive clauses. Treat that ambiguity as a review item rather than choosing a universal legal interpretation. Link [[Juris Guard Division]] and [[Decision Record Template]].
