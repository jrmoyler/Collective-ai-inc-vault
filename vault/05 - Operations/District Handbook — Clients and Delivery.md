# District Handbook — Clients and Delivery

## Purpose
Connect client readiness, onboarding, delivery, and dated billing evidence.

This is a virtual knowledge district. It preserves original note folders and creates a curated route through their related subjects. It does not activate a division or establish a physical facility.

## Work sequence
1. Read readiness evidence.
2. Inspect onboarding state.
3. Review delivery acceptance.
