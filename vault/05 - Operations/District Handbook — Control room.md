# District Handbook — Control room

## District purpose
Operating procedures turn knowledge into repeatable work. Task records hold assignment and state; notes hold the work product and its evidence.

Folder: 05 - Operations. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Read and claim a task, report presence, read existing notes, then append findings through the live API.
- Send work to review with a result that identifies changed notes and unresolved decisions.
- Tool connections need real authorization and observed health. A city terminal or sentinel does not grant external access.

## Required record fields
- Trigger and required inputs
- Procedure and authorized tools
- Expected outputs and acceptance check
- Failure recovery and escalation

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
