# District Handbook — Governance and Decisions

## Purpose
Inspect current governance, legal holds, decisions, and superseded authority records.

This is a virtual knowledge district. It preserves original note folders and creates a curated route through their related subjects. It does not activate a division or establish a physical facility.

## Work sequence
1. Read the current gate.
2. Check decision authority.
3. Record rationale and evidence.
