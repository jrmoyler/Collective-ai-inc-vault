# MCP-11 Growth Signal MCP

Custom MCP server 11 of 20 in the [[MCP Matrix]]. Owner: [[Signal Velocity Division]]. Also used by: Nexus Labs, ZenFlow. Status: to build.

[[Signal Velocity Division]] is one of the 9 operating divisions.

## Purpose

Signal Velocity paid media and growth intelligence MCP. Aggregates CAC, ROAS, LTV, and conversion data across Meta, Google, TikTok, and organic channels. Powers growth agent campaign decisions.
