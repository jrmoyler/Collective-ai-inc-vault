# Operating Principles and Source Reconciliation

## Eight operating principles, interpreted against current canon
1. Treat divisions as connected parts of one enterprise; document dependencies and handoffs.
2. Keep work aligned to its roadmap phase; later-phase concepts do not become current deliverables because they appear in a dossier.
3. Use the specified build platform or record architectural approval for a change.
4. Apply the current Aegis clearance before shipping; reviewed outputs need their approval record and held work needs its clearance.
5. Validate internal capabilities on internal work before making external delivery claims.
6. Route division intelligence through the shared ZenFlow architecture rather than assuming independent parallel infrastructure.
7. Reserve Founder and Co-Founder for JR Moyler and Devon Scott. Other titles describe actual functions.
8. Preserve Civic Core’s accountability and nonprofit mission without restoring the historical veto removed on October 1, 2026. See [[Civic Core Fiduciary Veto]].

## Current governance boundaries
The source includes historical permanence claims for Stanley Constant’s veto; current canon supersedes them. Helios Grid remains blocked pending the SEC legal opinion. A historical source cannot clear physical autonomy, clinical output or irreversible decisions. Use [[Aegis Protocol Spec]] and an actual approval record.

## Working conduct
Communicate concrete facts directly. Record exact observed errors and test results. Treat corrections as input to the architecture, produce reviewable finished work and change decisions when evidence changes. Separate the historical community/content cadence descriptions from present connection or staffing verification.

## Financial interpretation
ARR forecasts, phase-funding plans and market estimates describe intended outcomes. They do not replace the Sept 2026 record of zero paying customers and $0 MRR. No customer, sale or financial achievement is inferred from a source catalog.
