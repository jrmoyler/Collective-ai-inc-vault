# Operations Architect Delivery Scorecard

The onboarding packet describes three work domains: operations/talent, internal tooling, and community/learner support. Its automated examples and large membership counts are scenarios, not verified deployed services or current users.

## Proposed delivery arc
| Period | Focus | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Diagnose operational friction and actual tool handoffs | Observed workflow map and prioritized bottleneck |
| Days 31–60 | Build a bounded onboarding/internal-tool flow | Tested event, authorized access, error path and useful output |
| Days 61–90 | Extend proven flows and assess user progression | Measured adoption, cost/time change and review record |

## Source target scorecard
The packet proposes time-to-productivity under 14 days, more than 40 internal hours saved weekly, novice retention above 85% and community MAU growth of 20%. These are historical target examples. Set baselines and approved targets from actual operations before measuring improvement.

## Weekly evidence
Record actual new hires, client delivery state, community questions, shipped internal tooling, failures and one justified automation candidate. Named meeting times in the packet are proposed cadence, not booked calendar events.

## Controls
The packet's broad Super Admin language is not an access grant. Record approved scopes, secrets custody, review gates and tested failure cases. Automated messages/roles require an authorized destination and actual user consent. A prospective salary, option grant or incentive is not an executed agreement.
