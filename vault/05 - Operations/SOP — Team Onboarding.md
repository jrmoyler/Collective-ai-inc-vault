# SOP — Team Onboarding

> [!note] Proposed process from the training source
> This fills the team-onboarding gap recorded in [[SOP — Client Onboarding]]. It does not claim any person has completed training or received tool access.

## 1. Identity and role
Kenza's onboarding ownership is documented in the Personal Assistant Guide. Capture member name, actual assigned role, sponsor, relevant divisions and current work. Founder titles remain reserved to [[JR Moyler]] and [[Devon Scott]]. Use [[Role Assignment and Handoff Matrix]].

## 2. Shared foundation
Assign curriculum Weeks 1–6 to everyone. Establish operating-model understanding, safe source reading, product structure and initial build skills before specialization. Record the assigned source edition and evidence for each completed artifact.

## 3. Role track and proof
- Builder/engineer: Labs 03–08 plus deployed-site proof.
- Designer: brand/token rules, accessible component system and usable handoff.
- AI PM: requirements, workflow, acceptance rubric and delivery checklist.
- Agent engineer: grounded retrieval, evaluation bench and controlled tool access.
- Physical builder: bench-only prototype; movement/actuation requires Aegis-Hold review.
- Governance operator: review queue, permissions and audit evidence.

The curriculum allows PM/design/operator roles to skip deep ML implementation, while retaining model-evaluation and failure-mode understanding.

## 4. Access and first delivery
Record actual authorized tools and least necessary scopes. Do not infer access from a source catalog. First delivery should be inspectable: source links, acceptance tests, privacy/Aegis classification, user-facing output and reproducible verification.

## 5. Review
Score work against [[Artifact Review Rubric]]. Record reviewer, evidence, remaining gaps and next assigned artifact. External client output requires website/build QA and Aegis modules. Do not award completion for reading alone.

## Completion record
Member / sponsor / role / source edition / foundation artifacts / track artifacts / access approvals / reviewer / blockers / next assignment.
