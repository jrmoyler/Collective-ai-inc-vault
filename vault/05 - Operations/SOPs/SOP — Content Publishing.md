# SOP — Content Publishing

**Owner:** [[Director_Nexus_Labs]] (PRISM) with [[Justin Howell]] for community channels · **Applies to:** posts, ads, videos, newsletters, client marketing

## Before drafting
- [ ] Objective, audience, channel and call to action written in one line
- [ ] Client ads point the CTA to the client's own site (for example exclusiveessence.store)

## Draft check
- [ ] [[JR Voice Standard]]: no banned words, no warm-up opener, no summary closer
- [ ] Facts and numbers have a source
- [ ] Brand tokens from [[Design System Bible v3]] and the right division palette ([[Division Palettes]])
- [ ] Rights cleared for music, fonts, footage and likeness

## FTC compliance
- [ ] **Paid or gifted endorsements:** disclose the relationship in the caption, on screen and in the audio for video. A platform's "Paid partnership" tag alone is not enough
- [ ] **AI-generated endorsers:** when a synthetic person, AI avatar or cloned voice delivers a testimonial or product claim, disclose that the endorser is AI-generated. This applies to UGC-style ads built on an AI brand model
- [ ] **Reviews and testimonials:** never write fake reviews, never present an invented person's experience as real, never buy reviews. The 2024 FTC rule carries civil penalties above $53,000 per violation
- [ ] Health, income or results claims need proof before publishing

> [!warning] Flag for Exclusive Essence
> The current UGC plan says ads with the EE-model "must not look AI-generated." Under the FTC rules above, an AI persona giving a product testimonial needs a clear AI disclosure. Review with the client before the next ad runs. See [[Exclusive Essence]].

## Publish
- [ ] Final approval by the account owner
- [ ] Scheduled in the posting tool with UTM tags
- [ ] Logged with date, channel and link

## After 7 days
- [ ] Record reach, engagement, clicks and conversions in [[SignalBoard]] or the client report
