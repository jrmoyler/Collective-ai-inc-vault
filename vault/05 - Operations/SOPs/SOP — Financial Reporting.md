# SOP — Financial Reporting

**Owner:** [[Ahmad Muhammad]] (CFO) · **Agents:** [[Director_Quantum_Ledger]] (LEDGER) · **Cadence:** monthly close, quarterly review, annual filing

> [!note] Not legal or tax advice
> Built from public sources as of Oct 2026. Confirm with Collective AI's attorney and CPA before acting.

## Monthly close (by the 10th)
- [ ] Reconcile every bank and card account to statements
- [ ] Record all invoices issued and payments received in the [[Client Billing Ledger]]
- [ ] Categorize expenses by division so each division can carry its own P&L (dossier requirement)
- [ ] Accounts receivable aging. Chase anything over 30 days
- [ ] Monthly package: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, cash runway, MRR and paying-customer count
- [ ] Update [[Financial Position]]

## Quarterly
- [ ] Division P&L and KPI review for the operating divisions
- [ ] Federal estimated tax payments if the company expects to owe
- [ ] Payroll filings (Form 941) if anyone is on payroll
- [ ] Ohio CAT: no registration or return is needed while Ohio taxable gross receipts stay at or under $6 million a year. If the company opened a CAT account, it must still file quarterly returns (May 10, Aug 10, Nov 10, Feb 10)
- [ ] Update the cap table after any equity event ([[Cap Table Structure]])

## Annual
- [ ] Form 1099-NEC to every contractor paid $600 or more, by January 31
- [ ] Federal C-corporation return (Form 1120), due the 15th day of the 4th month after year end (April 15 for a calendar year)
- [ ] Ohio municipal net-profit return for Columbus
- [ ] Ohio statement of continued existence and agent filings as required by the Secretary of State
- [ ] Federal beneficial ownership (BOI) report: not required for U.S. companies under FinCEN's March 2025 interim final rule. Recheck each year

## Controls
- [ ] Two people see every payment over a set threshold
- [ ] Company money stays in company accounts. No mixing with personal accounts
- [ ] Civic Core money tracked separately from commercial divisions
