# SOP — New Division Launch Protocol

**Owner:** JR (founder authorization) · **Support:** [[Director_Operations]], [[Ahmad Muhammad]] · **Applies to:** moving a chartered division to operating, or a pending division (21–30) into the active roster

## Rule
A division is not active until JR authorizes it in writing. Until then nobody may describe it as staffed, launched, revenue-producing or available. Client work in that area goes through active divisions or qualified partners.

## Gate (all five must pass)
| Gate | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| G1 Leader | A named human lead who has accepted the role |
| G2 Demand | At least three external conversations showing a buyer will pay |
| G3 Regulation | Written map of licenses, export controls, privacy and industry rules, reviewed by [[Juris Guard Division]] |
| G4 Offer | One packaged offer with a price and delivery owner |
| G5 P&L | A standalone division budget and P&L line in [[Financial Position]] |

## Launch steps (from the dossier operating priorities)
1. Finalize the division charter and explicit non-overlap rules with neighboring divisions
2. Define 3–5 packaged offers with pricing and delivery ownership
3. Build a qualified external customer pipeline
4. Configure the 30-agent cluster and evaluation standards ([[SOP — Agent Deployment Checklist]])
5. Create the division P&L, KPI dashboard and quarterly operating review
6. Publish the division's Synergy Mandate contribution matrix for SOLOFORGE
