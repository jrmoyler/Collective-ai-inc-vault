## What Ohio law requires
Collective AI is an Ohio corporation (Ohio Rev. Code Chapter 1701). Its articles of incorporation must state the authorized number of shares, the par value per share (or that shares have no par value), each class and its express terms. Every share issued must fit inside what the articles authorize. If the articles authorized too few shares or only one class, amend them before issuing founder stock, options or preferred stock.

## Founder stock checklist
- [ ] Board consent approving each founder's stock purchase at a nominal price
- [ ] Stock purchase agreement with vesting. Common market practice is 4 years with a 1-year cliff, with acceleration terms decided up front
- [ ] IP assignment from each founder to the company (code, brand, ZenFlow architecture, dossiers)
- [ ] **83(b) election within 30 days** of receiving restricted stock. It can now be filed online with IRS Form 15620. Keep the confirmation page and give the company a copy. Large grants may need paper filing if they exceed the portal's limits
- [ ] Stock ledger entries and certificates or electronic notices

## Option pool
- [ ] Board-approved equity incentive plan with a share reserve
- [ ] Seed-stage benchmark: employee pools average about 12% of the company (Carta 2026)
- [ ] 409A valuation before granting options, refreshed every 12 months or after a financing
- [ ] Option grants under Rule 701 for employees and advisors

## Raising money
- **SAFEs:** the YC post-money SAFE is the common standard for pre-seed and seed. Model dilution before signing each one
- **Reg CF:** a company can raise up to $5 million in 12 months. Financial statement requirements step up with size (officer-certified, then CPA-reviewed, then audited for repeat issuers above the upper threshold). Note that the [[Kre8trix Reg CF Gate]] is Kre8trix Inc.'s raise, not Collective AI's
- **Benchmarks:** median founding teams keep about 56% after seed and 36% after Series A (Carta 2026)

## QSBS (Section 1202)
Collective AI is a C corporation, so its stock can qualify. For stock issued after July 4, 2025:
- 50% of gain excluded after 3 years, 75% after 4 years, 100% after 5 years
- Exclusion cap of $15 million per holder per company (or 10x basis)
- Company gross assets must stay at or under $75 million when the stock is issued
- The company must run an active, qualifying business

Record each holder's issue date and price so holding periods can be proven later.

## Entity questions to settle with counsel
- [ ] **Civic Core:** a C corporation division cannot itself hold 501(c)(3) status. Tax-exempt work usually needs a separate nonprofit corporation with its own board, and no shareholders
- [ ] **Civic Core veto:** if the removed fiduciary veto appears in any signed document, record an amendment or board resolution
- [ ] **Division equity:** dossiers say equity or revenue share is never automatic and is used only when justified. Keep it that way in partner and SOLOFORGE agreements
