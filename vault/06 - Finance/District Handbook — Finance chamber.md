# District Handbook — Finance chamber

## District purpose
Finance records distinguish dated actuals, forecasts, assumptions, and targets. September 2026 company actuals are zero paying customers and $0 MRR.

Folder: 06 - Finance. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Label source revenue figures as targets unless a current financial record verifies actual revenue.
- Record assumptions and confidence for scenario models; never fabricate a price, balance, or funding event.
- Mega Campus cost and power models are October 2026 estimates. The $6.54B figure belongs to the historical v3.0 model.

## Required record fields
- Measure and reporting period
- Actual, target, or estimate label
- Assumptions and source
- Decision and review evidence

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
