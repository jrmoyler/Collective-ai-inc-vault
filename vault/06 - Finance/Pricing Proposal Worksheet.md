# Pricing Proposal Worksheet

Use this worksheet to record a quotation, not to invent a new live rate card. Current priced offers and deposits in [[Revenue Plan — $21.7K per Month]] take precedence over examples from the 2025 framework.

## Deal record
| Field | Value to complete |
|---|---|
| Client and decision maker | Unknown until intake |
| Lead division and contributors | Domain owner plus named delivery participants |
| Outcome and acceptance test | Observable client result |
| Scope and exclusions | Included deliverables, quantities and limits |
| Budget band | Discovery answer and date |
| Approved offer/version | Airtable offer record or signed custom quote |
| Market comparison | Equivalent outcome, date, scope and source |
| DIY estimate | Hours, rates, tool costs and uncertainty |
| Complexity rationale | Integration and customization work |
| Integration premium rationale | Actual additional deliverables and client value |
| Price, currency and tax | Approved quote; tax treatment confirmed separately |
| Deposit and milestone schedule | Due dates and work-start condition |
| Rights | Client use, exclusivity, white-label and IP terms |
| Review record | CFO, CEO and contributing division approvals as required |

## Review before sending
- [ ] Price follows the current offer sheet or approved custom scope.
- [ ] Every multiplier is visible and justified.
- [ ] Scope reduction is written as fewer deliverables rather than a concealed rate discount.
- [ ] Each participating division has accepted its portion.
- [ ] Terms involving equity, revenue share, exclusivity or IP licensing have financial review.
- [ ] Acceptance, change orders and invoice identifiers are present.
- [ ] No forecast or quoted amount is described as collected revenue.

Link the approved proposal to [[SOP — Client Onboarding]] and [[Client Billing Ledger]].
