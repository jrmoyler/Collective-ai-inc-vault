# Universal Pricing Framework — Historical Logic

> [!warning] Historical policy, not today's offer sheet
> This source is v1.0 dated October 2025 and describes a 15-division model. Its division launch cohorts, CFO spelling variants and sample rates do not override current canon or priced offers. CFO is [[Ahmad Muhammad]]. See [[Financial Position]] and [[Revenue Plan — $21.7K per Month]].

## Calculation and inputs
| Field | Source meaning | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|
| Market Rate (MR) | Price of an equivalent client outcome | Comparable scope, date, source and currency |
| DIY Cost (DC) | Client's own tool, labor, learning and failed-attempt costs | Hours, labor rate, tool fees and assumptions |
| Client Budget Reality (CBR) | Selects a feasible service scope | Discovery budget band and approval owner |
| Collective AI Premium (CAP) | Added value of useful cross-division integration | Named divisions, outputs and documented client benefit |

The source formula is SSP = (MR + DC) / 2. It then applies complexity and budget modifiers. Page 7 also introduces integration multipliers; the source does not provide one universal worked equation that includes every CAP combination. Record the chosen combination and obtain review instead of silently stacking terms.

## Historical modifiers
| Complexity | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Standard | 1.0 |
| Custom | 1.2 |
| Complex | 1.5 |
| Bespoke | 1.75–2.0 |
| Enterprise exclusive | 2.0+ |
| Crisis or urgent | 1.3 |

| Integration | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| One division | 1.0 |
| Two-division bundle | 1.15 |
| Three-plus division bundle | 1.25 |
| Direct ZenFlow core access | 1.2 |
| Exclusive portfolio | 1.4 |
| IP or white-label rights | 1.5–2.0 |

These are historical framework values, not prices currently offered to customers.

## Guardrails recorded in the source
1. Keep the DIY cost as a floor and justify any charge above the market comparison.
2. Reduce scope when budget is insufficient rather than hide a discount.
3. Ask the budget range during initial discovery.
4. Show every multiplier and its reason to the client.
5. Route enterprise, equity/revenue-share and licensing terms for CFO review.
6. Get each contributing division lead's scope and price sign-off.
7. Refresh comparable market inputs quarterly; reassess if three or more losses cite price.
8. Record exceptions with CEO and CFO approval.

## Source governance and acquisition protocol
The source assigns framework ownership and enterprise review to the CFO, market inputs to division leads, AI integration logic to Dr. Joseph Johnson, and version notices to Kenza. It calls for annual framework review and a 90-day catalog-mapping window after an acquisition closes. No acquisition is asserted here.

The source excludes Civic Core from SSP and describes free community services supported by grants, portfolio allocations and in-kind contributions. Keep this as the historical funding model, with [[Civic Core Fiduciary Veto]] documenting later governance changes.
