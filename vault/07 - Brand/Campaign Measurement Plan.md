# Campaign Measurement Plan

The March 29 campaign proposes measurements across the funnel. Record actual numbers with a time window and denominator; keep historical targets separate.

| Asset | Measures |
|---|---|
| Blog | Organic sessions, reading time, 50%/80% scroll events, keyword positions and backlinks |
| Landing page | Unique visitors, conversions, conversion rate and source breakdown |
| Horizontal video | Views, average watched fraction, end-screen click-through and relevant inquiries |
| Vertical video | Views, completion, saves, profile visits and followers gained |
| Email | Opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes and booked conversations |
| Skool | New members, weekly active members and contributions |

## Experiment record
Hypothesis / variants / audience / start/end / asset revision / primary outcome / conversion definition / attribution method / results / confounders / decision.

The source proposes a 30-day headline comparison across blog, LinkedIn, TikTok and email. Cross-channel results are directional rather than a controlled same-audience experiment. Record those differences before choosing a winner.

## Historical targets
The source proposes landing-page conversion of 8–15%, a 35% first-email open rate, three to five booked conversations and 25 new community members in 30 days. These are planning targets from March 2026, never company results or current benchmarks.

Review actual results before scheduling refreshes. Link [[Content Campaign Production System]], [[Campaign Evidence Register]] and [[Financial Position]] so marketing outcomes are not confused with collected revenue.
