# Campaign Publication Checklist

The March 2026 campaign's operational checks remain useful; platform algorithm recommendations must be rechecked at publication time.

## Every asset
- [ ] Final headline and length checked against the destination.
- [ ] Exactly one requested action.
- [ ] Links tested and campaign attribution parameters recorded.
- [ ] Captions, descriptions and alt text finished.
- [ ] Publish timing and first-hour response owner assigned from current evidence.
- [ ] Claims and examples have source/date/type in [[Campaign Evidence Register]].

## Channel-specific delivery
| Channel | Source production checks |
|---|---|
| Blog | Metadata, short relevant URL, H1/H2, applicable FAQ/schema, useful internal links, primary citations, mobile/performance QA |
| LinkedIn | Native video where selected, caption file, strong opening, prepared destination link, response owner |
| YouTube | Deliberate thumbnail, chapter markers starting at 0:00, useful description, end-screen action and relevant category |
| Instagram | Vertical framing, caption matching the narrative and tested destination link |
| TikTok | 1080×1920 MP4 without unintended bars, readable opening hook, finished caption and original-audio attribution |
| Email | Subject/preview compatibility, one action, unsubscribe path and plain-text/HTML parity |

The source's exact hashtag counts, posting-time claims and expected platform reach are historical campaign assumptions. They do not establish current limits, ranking rules or guaranteed performance.

## Approval record
Final files / source review / owner / destination / privacy classification / authorization to publish / observed link/form results / publication revision / next measurement date.

No testimonial, customer result, scarcity claim or community conversation may be fabricated. Link [[SOP — Content Publishing]] and [[Content Campaign Production System]].
