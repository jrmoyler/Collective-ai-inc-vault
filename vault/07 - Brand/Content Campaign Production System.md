# Content Campaign Production System

> [!note] Historical campaign, reusable production structure
> The source was prepared March 29, 2026. Its market statistics, platform claims and performance goals are historical inputs, not current verified facts or achieved campaign results.

## Asset contract
The master deliverable contains five linked sections: research intelligence, long-form article, landing-page copy/design brief, video script/Remotion brief, and distribution plan. Its audience journey is pain → failure mechanism → credible path → proof → identity → one action.

## Distribution architecture
| Layer | Job | Next destination |
|---|---|---|
| Blog | Search discovery and detailed explanation | Landing page or lead magnet |
| Video | Awareness and demonstrated narrative | Blog or landing page |
| Landing page | One defined conversion | Email list |
| Email | Useful follow-up before sales ask | Discovery conversation |
| Skool | Ongoing retention and questions | Future research/content |

## Production record
Topic / audience problem / source claims and dates / evidence quality / blog outline / landing-page action / video duration/aspect ratios / approved voiceover / original assets / captions / accessible descriptions / distribution owners / attribution links / analytics events.

## Source integrity
The original campaign repeats AI-failure percentages and scarcity copy. Neither is safe to reuse as a present fact without checking the underlying sources and actual capacity. Distinguish measured evidence, forecasts, opinions and the campaign's own claims. Use [[Campaign Evidence Register]] before publication.

Use [[Campaign Publication Checklist]] for shipping and [[Campaign Measurement Plan]] for outcomes. This note authorizes no publishing or messages.
