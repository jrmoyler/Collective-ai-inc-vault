# District Handbook — Brand atelier

## District purpose
Brand work follows current voice and visual standards. Source assets and dated approvals determine what can be used.

Folder: 07 - Brand. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Read JR Voice Standard and current brand records before writing or producing assets.
- Use the recorded division identity and palette. Do not borrow an accent simply because a scene would look brighter.
- Keep claims tied to observed capability. Distinguish future plans from current operating products.

## Required record fields
- Audience and purpose
- Voice and design references
- Source asset and usage scope
- Approval record and delivery evidence

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
