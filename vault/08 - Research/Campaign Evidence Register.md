# Campaign Evidence Register

## Claim record
| Field | Capture |
|---|---|
| Proposed claim | Exact wording intended for publication |
| Primary source | Original study/report URL, not only an internal summary |
| Source date and population | Publication date, sample and domain |
| Evidence type | Measured result, survey, estimate, prediction or opinion |
| Denominator and definition | What counts as failure, adoption, ROI or completion |
| Limitations | Method, exclusions and uncertainty |
| Internal use | Asset/section where it appears |
| Review status | Unverified, qualified, approved or removed |
| Reviewer and date | Actual review record |

## Source-specific caution
The March 2026 campaign attributes adoption/failure statistics to McKinsey, MIT/Fortune, Gartner, RAND and other sources. Those references are leads for verification, not evidence that the vault has checked every original paper. Preserve the internal document's date and do not combine percentages from different populations into one market claim.

Case studies require a real underlying engagement and permission. An illustrative scenario must say it is illustrative. Availability claims require actual capacity. Predictions must remain predictions.

Use [[Evidence Record Template]] for deeper research and [[Campaign Publication Checklist]] for release.
