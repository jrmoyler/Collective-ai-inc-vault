# District Handbook — Learning and Curriculum

## Purpose
Work through curricula, learning products, studybooks, and assessed practice.

This is a virtual knowledge district. It preserves original note folders and creates a curated route through their related subjects. It does not activate a division or establish a physical facility.

## Work sequence
1. Read a curriculum.
2. Practice against an assessment.
3. Record evidence and feedback.
