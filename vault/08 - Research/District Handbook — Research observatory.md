# District Handbook — Research observatory

## District purpose
Research notes preserve questions, sources, findings, uncertainty, and implications. Separate what a source says from an inference about Collective AI.

Folder: 08 - Research. This handbook describes the virtual knowledge district, not a physical campus district.

## Work kit
- Capture author or institution, publication date, source URL, and the question investigated.
- Separate peer-reviewed work, preprints, vendor documentation, and internal observations.
- Connect a finding to a relevant division and decision. Tool documentation in older matrices is a design reference, not current integration proof.

## Required record fields
- Research question
- Source and evidence type
- Findings and inference boundary
- Unknowns and follow-up test

## Completion check
- The source and its effective date are recorded.
- Current facts and historical targets are distinct.
- Linked division and hub notes exist.
- The next action or unresolved question is stated.
- An existing note is extended before a duplicate is created.
