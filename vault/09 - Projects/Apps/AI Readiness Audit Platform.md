## Features

- Landing page with hero, stats and trust signals.
- 25-question multi-step audit in 6 sections with localStorage persistence.
- Claude-powered instant assessment and a Recharts results dashboard.
- Calendly prefill for high-score leads; admin CRM with filters, sorting and CSV export.
- Supabase backend with row-level security.

## Tech stack

Next.js 14, Tailwind, Recharts, Anthropic SDK, Supabase.
