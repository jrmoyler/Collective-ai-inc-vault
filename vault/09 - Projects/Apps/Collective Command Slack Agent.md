## Features

- Executive Q&A, decision briefs and company knowledge.
- Project planning, status synthesis, next-step tracking, risk and dependency call-outs.
- Lead intake, discovery prep, qualification and follow-up drafts.
- Meeting prep, agendas, notes cleanup and action registers; writing for Slack, email, proposals and site copy.
- Instructions forbid inventing project status, owners, dates, customer details or financial results.
