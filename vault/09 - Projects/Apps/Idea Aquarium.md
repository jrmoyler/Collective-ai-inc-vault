# Idea Aquarium

A living habitat for venture concepts: startup ideas, MVPs and creative concepts rendered as aquatic organisms in a digital tank. Motion, size, glow and behavior encode synergy, revenue, joy, complexity, novelty and momentum.

## Features

- 18 idea organisms on a Three.js WebGL canvas as soft-bodied bioluminescent lifeforms.
- Body plans by strategic species: drifters (jellyfish), swarmers (larvae), floaters (glowing sacs), hunters (cephalopods).
- Dormant ideas sink and dim; high-momentum ideas pulse more; high-revenue ideas glow amber; related ideas co-drift.
- Seeded local mock data only, no backend.

## Tech stack

React + Vite + TypeScript, Three.js, Framer Motion, Anime.js, Tailwind.
