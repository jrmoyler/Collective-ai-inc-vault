# Collective AI Mega Campus

Collective AI's planned AI and technology campus in Columbus, Ohio. Concept stage. Lead: [[JR Moyler]] (Hataalii). **JR confirmed the Sept 16, 2026 register as canonical on Oct 4, 2026.**

## Canonical facts (Sept 16, 2026 register)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Site | 220 acres; schematic site 3,300 x 2,904 ft (1,005.84 m x 885.14 m) |
| Facilities | 35 (CF-01 to CF-35): 25 for operating and chartered divisions and shared use, 10 for pending divisions |
| Design area | 2,045,000 sq ft (164,000 of it is a study allowance for CF-24 and CF-25) |
| Floors | 74 |
| Room programs | 444 (six per floor) |
| Districts | 6: Brain and Governance, Knowledge and Media, Manufacturing and Logistics, Living Systems, Public and Community, Energy and Mesh |
| Connected systems (explorer) | 10 mesh spires; energy paths from the central utility plant (CF-21) to every facility; solar, wind, grid and storage layer; about 290 moving agents (shuttles, freight, androids, drones, people); 7 connected lakes |
| Power model | Grid-connected; not self-powered. No MW or GWh figure in the register |
| Development cost | Not stated in the register. $6.54B is a v3.0 figure, not restated in the Sept 16 register |
| Status | Proposed; no site control or financing recorded |
| Source | Repo `data/campus-program.json` + `viewer/campus/data.js` (220-acre campus package, S13/S14/S16 documents, CF-01 to CF-35 infographics) |

Placement follows the full-expansion artwork (Campus Variant 29, with variants 17, 04 and 41); architecture follows each CF infographic. Room furnishing is interpreted, not surveyed.

## Cost and power (Oct 2026 estimates)

Concept-class estimates built Oct 4, 2026 from published 2025–2026 benchmarks. All figures are estimates; the register itself gives none, and the $6.54B / 96 MW / 150 MW v3.0 figures are superseded.

| Metric | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Total development cost | $1.05B–$2.30B (about $515–$1,123/sq ft all-in) |
| Building hard cost | $686M–$1.43B |
| Land (220 acres) | $24M–$64M at $110k–$290k/acre |
| Neural Block (CF-02) critical IT | 9–21 MW, $88M–$420M |
| Campus annual electricity | 96 GWh–271 GWh |
| Campus peak load | 18–44 MW |
| On-site solar + CHP share | roughly 64–71% of annual energy (matched low/high cases); grid firms everything |
