# District Handbook — Facilities and Infrastructure

## Purpose
Explore facility programs, infrastructure dependencies, and dated campus estimates.

This is a virtual knowledge district. It preserves original note folders and creates a curated route through their related subjects. It does not activate a division or establish a physical facility.

## Work sequence
1. Read a facility program.
2. Check the current register.
3. Inspect infrastructure assumptions.
