# The Architect's Ascendance

A retro cyber-mystic browser JRPG ("Architecting a Humane Future") built with vanilla HTML5 Canvas and ES modules. The Nexus has fractured because the five divisions stopped listening to each other. The four leaders are Collective AI people: Hataalii the Architect, Devon the Scout, Ahmad the Strategist and Kenza the Orchestrator.

## Features

- **Main quest, The Shard Triad**: Civic Hall (Oracle Maren), Kinetic Arena (Kinetic Shard), Grand Exchange (Debt Dragon, Quantum Shard), Hybrid Academy (Lumen Blossoms, Division Shard), ZenFlow Core (Void Titan).
- **Outer Ring** post-game: warp to ten more divisions (Vital Helix, Binary Loom, Gaia Synthesis, VectorShift, Animus Prime, Aether Link, Obsidian Arc, Terra Axis, Nexus Labs, The Collective), each with a guardian, seal and steward. The Collective Atlas lists fifteen divisions.
- Companions Stanley, Dr. Joseph, Denzel and Arthur join as guardians fall. Party of four; per-leader prologues, briefings and endings.
- Enemies such as the Entropy Specter, Market Crash Goblin, Data Breach Wraith and Stagnation Golem.
- Recent additions (Sept 30, 2026): 13 playable Division Trials, Forge / rune table / alchemist / contracts vendors, 54 post-game legendary items, the optional Fragmentation Phoenix superboss.
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; CRT filter; installable web manifest; Playwright smoke tests.

## Tech stack

Vanilla Canvas + ES modules, Vite, ESLint, Playwright.
