# EnerGenius CHP Microgrid

**EnerGenius** is the canon name for the campus CHP microgrid of the [[Collective AI Mega Campus]]. In the Sept 16 register it lives in [[EnerGenius + Helios Central Utility Plant]] (CF-21) with [[Helios Grid]] solar.

## What the Sept 16 package supports

- CF-21 room programs: microgrid switchgear, battery interface, cooling plant, thermal equipment, water reuse, utility control.
- Reference artwork: battery arrays, three horizontal turbines (cogeneration), two exhaust stacks, five vertical water-treatment vessels, cooling fan housings and a glazed command room. The audit notes these are visually inferred, not a construction or operating design.
- CF-34 Energy Commons connects to this plant; it is not a second plant.
- S13 program card for CF-21: natural gas turbines, BESS, solar inverters, absorption chilling, heat recovery, water reclamation, utility SCADA; owner line Terra Axis + Binary Loom.

No generator ratings, storage capacity or self-sufficiency figure appears in the register. EnerGenius is part of a grid-connected campus, not a self-powered one. Earlier EnerGenius design studies (Q1 2026, v2.0, Ghana) are summarized in [[Mega Campus Version History]].
