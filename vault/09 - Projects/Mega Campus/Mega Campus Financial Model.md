# Mega Campus Financial Model

Financial picture of the [[Collective AI Mega Campus]] under the canonical Sept 16, 2026 register. Figures here are plans, not results; Collective AI had 0 paying customers and $0 MRR as of Sept 2026.

> [!warning] No cost model for the canonical register yet
> The Sept 16 package contains no costs, revenue, IRR or phasing. The **$6.54B total development cost is a v3.0 figure, not restated in the Sept 16 register** and was sized for 180 acres / ~4.9M GSF / 30 facilities, not the register's 220 acres / 2,045,000 sq ft / 35 facilities. A new model is needed before quoting any campus cost.
