# Mega Campus Mobility and Logistics

Logistics, transit and circulation of the [[Collective AI Mega Campus]]. **VectorShift** is the canon term for autonomous logistics ([[VectorShift Division]]).

## What the Sept 16 package supports

- [[Vector Hub]] (CF-09, 150,000 sq ft): cross-dock terminal, freight dispatch, battery swap, vehicle maintenance, drone ground support, logistics control; the reference shows a rounded horseshoe deck with twenty roof drone ports and a central control tower.
- [[Materials + Inventory Warehouse]] (CF-10): receiving, racked inventory, pick-pack, returns.
- [[Nomad Nexus Global Mobility Hub]] (CF-18): field kits and deployment planning.
- [[Visitor, Security and Campus Operations Center]] (CF-22): shuttle dispatch.
- Explorer: curved outer ring road, autonomous shuttles, freight trucks, androids, drones and pedestrians on defined paths; kinetic guidance strips embedded in asphalt (visual).
- Related products: [[Ground Vector]], [[Sky Vector]].

Earlier fleet sizes (35 ground units, 60+ VTOL drones) and the v3.0 road widths are in [[Mega Campus Version History]].
