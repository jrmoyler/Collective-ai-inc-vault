# Mega Campus Power and Data Center Infrastructure

Power and data-center picture for the [[Collective AI Mega Campus]] under the canonical Sept 16, 2026 register.

> [!info] Power model
> The campus is not self-powered. The explorer's energy layer shows solar, wind, **grid** and storage, and the source package says the [[The Energy Commons — Volta Grid]] connects to the existing utility plant rather than adding a second plant. The register itself gives no MW, MWh or GWh figures.

## What the Sept 16 package supports

- [[EnerGenius + Helios Central Utility Plant]] (CF-21, 50,000 sq ft): microgrid switchgear, battery interface, cooling plant, thermal equipment, water reuse, utility control. Reference artwork shows battery arrays, horizontal turbines/cogeneration, exhaust stacks and water-treatment vessels.
- [[The Neural Block]] (CF-02, 60,000 sq ft, 2 floors): GPU data hall, power distribution, liquid cooling, network rooms, mission control.
- [[Kinetic Energy Operations Center (KEOC)]] (CF-24): kinetic systems test and paving sensor lab; area and ownership unrecovered.
- [[Gaia Synthesis Bio-Energy Center]] (CF-25): circular systems, algae research, water polishing and bioenergy research pilots; area and ownership unrecovered.
- [[The Energy Commons — Volta Grid]] (CF-34, pending division): energy demonstration and grid intelligence, tied to the existing plant.
- Explorer layers: "Solar · wind · grid · storage" and "Heat recovery · bio-energy · kinetic"; gold energy paths from CF-21 to every facility; a ring of 12 wind turbines and solar fields drawn as scenery (visual, no capacity stated).

The Sept 16 Neural Block (60,000 sq ft, 2 floors) is far smaller than the v3.0 data center (1,036,800 sf), so the v3.0 power figures should not be carried forward without a new engineering basis. Kinetic paving and algae remain secondary and educational systems.
