## Changes from v3.0 to the Sept 16 register

- **Acreage** 180 to 220; **facilities** 30 to 35 (including 10 pending-division expansion buildings); **area** ~4.9M GSF to 2,045,000 sq ft.
- **Districts** changed from Utility and Data / Governance and Knowledge / Public and Wellness / Manufacturing and Logistics / Bioenergy, Farm and Life-Science / Visitor, Hotel, Mobility and Residential to Brain and Governance / Knowledge and Media / Manufacturing and Logistics / Living Systems / Public and Community / Energy and Mesh.
- **Building programs** now follow the divisions. The v3.0 data had three mismatches (Signal Velocity Center described as telecom, Eon Core Systems House as systems integration, Nomad Nexus Mobility Lab as autonomous mobility); the register's Growth War Room, Longevity Research Pavilion and Global Mobility Hub resolve them.
- **New in the register, absent from v3.0**: [[Glyph Forge Works]], [[Materials + Inventory Warehouse]], [[Terra Axis Living Systems Yard]], [[Employee Commons and Wellness Village]] and CF-26 to CF-35.
- **v3.0 buildings dropped**: Observatory and Sky Deck (32,000 sf), Forge Materials Lab (108,000), Habitat Eco-Residential Commons (322,000), Nexus Transportation Hub (126,000), Sentinel Security Command (75,000), Foundry Manufacturing District (450,000), Construction Innovation Yard (100,000), Grand Conference Hotel and Innovation Center (268,800). Visitor and Experience Center and Emergency Operations Center merged into CF-22.
- **Sizes**: v3.0 buildings were larger (Neural Block 1,036,800 sf and 96 MW critical IT vs CF-02 at 60,000 sq ft; Prism 180,000 vs 80,000).
- **Power and cost figures** from v3.0 are not restated in the register; see [[Mega Campus Power and Data Center Infrastructure]] and [[Mega Campus Financial Model]].

## Earlier system specs (superseded designs)

- **EnerGenius**: Q1 2026, 2 x 5 MW net Taurus 70-class CHP, 4.5 MW DC solar, 20 MWh LFP, 120 ms islanding, load-shed tiers 1-3. v2.0, 2 x 3.5 MW turbines, 3.2-5.0 MW solar, 8-12 MWh BESS, 10 x 750 kW diesel, 2 x 1,200-ton absorption chillers, 300,000 gal/day water reuse. Ghana, ~30 MW CHP, ~25 MWac PV, 60 MWh / 20 MW BESS.
- **Aether Link (Q1 2026)**: five 300 ft spires, 400G fiber ring, N78 sub-6 GHz outdoor, WiFi 6E indoor, 50,000+ IoT sensors, dedicated 5G slice for Clone Alpha (10 ms RTT), per-spire edge compute, zero-trust, $750K-$1.5M per spire.
- **Clone Alpha (Q1 2026)**: 50 units in Phase 1 (200+ by Year 5); 5'8"-6'0", 90-110 kg; 48 DOF; 80 kWh solid-state pack, 10 h; Jetson Orin 275 TOPS; 25 kg carry; IP54; 22 kW dock. Nexus Hub, 25,000 sf, 50 charging pedestals, OTA update server.
- **VectorShift (Q1 2026)**: 35 Ground Vector units, 60+ Sky Vector VTOL drones (40 kg, 25 miles), 12 verti-ports (v2.0 said 20; the register's Vector Hub artwork shows twenty).
- **Security zones (v2.0)**: Zone 0 public, 1 campus, 2 restricted, 3 secure, 4 industrial, mapped to Aegis-Clear / Review / Hold.
- **v2.0 financial structure**: phases 0-5 (land, revenue core, manufacturing spine, physical AI, biosphere, expansion); capital sources (sponsor equity, Series A, Opportunity Zone, EB-5, NMTC, senior loan, USDA/SBA 504, Ohio incentives, IRA credits, mezzanine); go/no-go gates (AEP power commitment, ESA, zoning pre-app, Series A close, GMP contract, colocation pre-leasing, FAA clearance).
- **v3.0 site data**: 1,097 m x 664 m; modified hex grid; roads 18 m perimeter, 22 m boulevard, 14 m secondary, 8 m promenade; North and East River Edges, 60 m reflecting pool, four algae ponds.
- **Sept 10 single-file anatomy map** (v3.0, 30 hotspots): fixes included inlining THREE.js, a duplicate CSS `display`, zero-size hotspot wrappers, the drive camera inside road geometry, isometric clipping, and replacing a generated schematic with the real artwork.

## Primary Drive editions disposition — 2026-10-06
| Drive edition | Historical scope | Current treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Master Technical Breakdown, Q1 2026 | 150 acres, 630,000 sq ft, thirteen facilities, five districts | Historical source; timeline above already preserves it |
| Updated Mega Campus Blueprint v2, June 2026 | 180-acre recommendation with 150 minimum/220 future-proof; approximately 1.05–1.20M sq ft target | Superseded planning edition |
| Master Development Dossier v2, June 2026 | 180-acre campus and planning-cost assumptions | Superseded cost/development study |

The v2 cost dossier explicitly labels cost figures planning-level estimates, uses a ±20% range, and calls its 0.94 regional factor an assumption requiring validation. The approximate targets in the source do not replace the September register's exact program.

Current canon remains 220 acres, 35 facilities, 2,045,000 sq ft and six physical campus districts. Current vault navigation districts are a separate system. Helios Grid remains blocked pending an SEC legal opinion. Neither an older energy table nor a facility concept authorizes deployment.

The three sources have been accounted for as historical supporting material rather than imported as a competing live campus.
