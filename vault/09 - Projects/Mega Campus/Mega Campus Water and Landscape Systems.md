# Mega Campus Water and Landscape Systems

Water and landscape of the [[Collective AI Mega Campus]] in the Sept 16 package.

## What the package supports

- Seven connected reflecting lakes and water gardens; dense planting with pink flowering clusters; branch-and-leaf foliage with planting setbacks; glass, water and foliage do not glow at dusk (DESIGN.md).
- [[The Water Observatory — Aqua Meridian]] (CF-28, pending division): water treatment pilots and water science; the audit says no operating capacity is asserted.
- [[EnerGenius + Helios Central Utility Plant]] (CF-21): water reuse.
- [[Eden Spire]] (CF-11): aeroponic growing, nutrient plant, algae panels.
- [[Gaia Synthesis Bio-Energy Center]] (CF-25): water polishing and algae research pilots.

Algae systems are research and educational, not bulk power. The v3.0 water features (North and East River Edges, 60 m reflecting pool, four algae ponds) are earlier site data; see [[Mega Campus Version History]].
