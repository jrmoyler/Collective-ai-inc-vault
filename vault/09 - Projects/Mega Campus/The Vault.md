## S13 program card

The register cites the "S13 program" for this facility: card #03 *The Vault* in the June 2026 Updated Blueprint v2.0 (owner line: Quantum Ledger + Obsidian Arc). The register's area and floor count govern; the card supplies function and intent.

| Field | S13 program |
|---|---|
| Function | Quantum Ledger treasury, Obsidian Arc security operations, cold storage, risk command, cyber-physical defense. |
| Architectural features | Blast-proof concrete shell; circular underground vault; Watchtower AI command; biometric airlocks; restricted access core. |
| Infrastructure | Hardened VLANs, Faraday-shielded rooms, cold storage, camera analytics, incident response room, secure evidence route to Juris Guard. |
| Governance / safety | Aegis-Hold for live financial execution, securities, irreversible custody actions. |
