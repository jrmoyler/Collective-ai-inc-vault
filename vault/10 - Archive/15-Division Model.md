## Drive source expansion — 2026-10-06

## Confidential Overview source disposition
The primary Drive Confidential Overview describes fifteen divisions and five launch cohorts. Its source thesis treats shared intelligence, infrastructure, creative support and financial rails as a connected portfolio, with a Solo Corporation model serving independent operators.

This is historical context. It does not reinstate fifteen as the current division count, declare a funded launch cohort, establish Civic Core tax-exempt registration or prove self-financing results. Current canon is twenty divisions, nine operating and eleven chartered, with pending 21–30 unactivated.

The source names JR Moyler and Devon Scott as co-founders, consistent with current canon. The present source map and operating rules live in [[Source Corpus Reading Map]], [[Financial Position]] and [[000 — Collective AI Knowledge Base]].
