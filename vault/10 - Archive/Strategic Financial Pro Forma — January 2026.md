# Strategic Financial Pro Forma — January 2026

> [!warning] Forecast artifact, not actual performance
> This document is dated January 2026 and presents a 2025–2035 strategy. It predates the current financial position and must not be used as a record of sales, customers, assets or company valuation.

## Historical thesis
Years 1–3 use strategy and education services to fund R&D and planned physical infrastructure. Years 4–10 propose a shift toward recurring software and utility-like infrastructure revenue. Those are strategic assumptions, not activated business units.
