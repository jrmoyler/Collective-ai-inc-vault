## What it is

Binary Loom's dual-printer fabrication workstation for rapid enclosure iteration, cable brackets, PCB holders, and custom chassis parts. Managed by a Pi 5 print-queue controller.

## Outcome

Full fabrication bench — enclosures, brackets, chassis, and shells for every Foundry node.

## Hardware (bill of materials)

- [ ] Bambu Lab A1 (fast prototyping)

- [ ] Raspberry Pi 5 (print queue controller)
- [ ] Heat-set inserts + M2/M3 screw kit
- [ ] PLA/PETG/TPU/ASA filament inventory
