## What it is

A low-cost wearable badge kit distributed to community program participants. Captures attendance, engagement signals, and session interactions — all stored locally on the Civic Core Mac mini.

## Outcome

Community engagement badges — attendance tracking, BLE logging, haptic confirmation, local data only.
