## What it is

A portable Civic Core field kit for off-site digital equity events. Battery-backed Pi 5 shell, mesh comms, and a speaker/mic setup for group demos and workshops at community centers, schools, and libraries.

## Outcome

Off-site AI demo kit — battery-powered, mesh-connected, group voice sessions, no internet required.
