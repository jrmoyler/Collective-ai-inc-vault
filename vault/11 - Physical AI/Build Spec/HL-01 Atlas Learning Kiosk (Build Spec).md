## What it is

A voice-first student learning terminal built from a Pi 5, Whisplay display, far-field mic, and local AI model inference. Works without internet by routing through the Foundry local cloud.

## Outcome

Student AI learning terminal — voice-first, offline-capable, local AI tutoring, session logging.
