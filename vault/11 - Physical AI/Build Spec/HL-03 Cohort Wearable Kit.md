## What it is

A cohort of wearable BLE sensor badges distributed to students during intensive sessions. Captures engagement signals (motion, environment, audio proximity) and logs to the local Hybrid Living Mac mini.

## Outcome

Cohort engagement sensing — BLE motion/environment capture, attention signal logging, session analytics.

## Hardware (bill of materials)

- [ ] Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2 (×6 per cohort)
- [ ] Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense (×6)
- [ ] Adafruit DRV2605L Haptic Controller (×6)
- [ ] Circuit Playground Bluefruit (×6)
- [ ] PowerBoost 1000 Charger + LiPo (×6)
- [ ] 3D-printed Bambu A1 badge shells (TPU)
