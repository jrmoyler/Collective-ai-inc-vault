## What it is

A portable Pi 5 field terminal with far-field mic, camera, and mesh comms for sideline coaching data capture. Syncs athlete stats to the Kinetic Edge Mac mini and broadcasts real-time performance overlays.

## Outcome

Sideline coaching terminal — athlete stat capture, mesh comms, real-time performance briefings.
