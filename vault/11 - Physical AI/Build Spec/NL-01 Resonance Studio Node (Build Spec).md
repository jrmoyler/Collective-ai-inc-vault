## What it is

A professional-grade audio capture and processing station for podcast recording, voice AI training datasets, and branded content production. Beamforming mic array, I2S amplifier, and Jetson-based noise processing in a custom-printed shell.

## Outcome

Podcast/voice capture station — beamforming array, local noise processing, session archive to NAS.
