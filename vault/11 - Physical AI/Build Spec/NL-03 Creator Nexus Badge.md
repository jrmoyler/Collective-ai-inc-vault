## What it is

A BLE wearable for Nexus Labs creators that captures ambient session audio, logs inspiration moments with a single haptic press, and uploads to the local knowledge vault at session end.

## Outcome

Creator wearable — ambient audio capture, inspiration logging, BLE upload to knowledge vault.
