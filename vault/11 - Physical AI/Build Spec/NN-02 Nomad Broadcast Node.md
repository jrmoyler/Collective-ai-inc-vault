## What it is

A portable audio/video capture node for remote content creation, client calls, and field broadcasts. Battery-backed, mesh-connected, and capable of uploading session recordings to the Foundry NAS on return.

## Outcome

Portable field studio — remote content capture, mesh uplink, session archive on NAS return.
