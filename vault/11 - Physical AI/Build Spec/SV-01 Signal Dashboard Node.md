## What it is

A wall-mounted Pi node that displays live funnel metrics, ad spend, and conversion data from the Signal Velocity Mac mini as an ambient performance dashboard.

## Outcome

Ambient marketing dashboard — live funnel metrics, voice queries, audio performance alerts.

## Hardware (bill of materials)

- [ ] Whisplay HAT

- [ ] ReSpeaker 2-Mics HAT (voice query)
- [ ] Adafruit I2S Speaker Bonnet
