## What it is

A smart wearable badge for consultants that captures ambient session audio, logs key phrases via BLE to the nearest Pi gateway, and serves as a physical access credential for Collective AI systems.

## Outcome

Smart consulting credential — ambient session capture, BLE logging, haptic feedback, access token.
