## What it is

A wearable neural-signal capture prototype built from a BLE sensor board with IMU and haptic feedback. Captures motion, orientation, and environmental context for cognitive performance tracking.

## Outcome

Cognitive performance wearable — motion + orientation capture, haptic feedback, BLE health uplink.
