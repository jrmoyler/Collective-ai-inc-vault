# Foundry Infrastructure Hardware Catalog

Internal Collective AI catalog of **105 non-wearable physical AI hardware products**: five distinct items for the parent company and five for each of the 20 divisions. It covers control panels, smart furniture, camera rigs, environmental fixtures, storage, charging docks, safety hardware, test jigs, displays and field infrastructure. Ordinary nodes, terminals, field kits, badges and wrist monitors are excluded. 47 pages.

> [!info] Headline numbers
> - Items: 105 (21 entities x 5)
> - Combined budget, one unit of each item: **$26,920 – $74,530**
> - Per-unit items counted once: [[Perimeter Motion Stake]] (each), [[Environmental Sensor Stake]] (each), [[Privacy Shutter Array]] (per camera)
> - Phases: the catalog assigns no build phases or per-item IDs. Every item is a prototype concept under staged Aegis-Hold/Aegis-Review.

> [!warning] Status
> These are catalog concepts, not built or deployed hardware. 11 of the 20 divisions are chartered, not operating.

## Phase summary

The catalog does not split items into purchase phases. Its governance page says it draws on the Physical AI Foundry Build Spec purchase phases, but it does not map items to them. All 105 items share one gate: Aegis-Hold until testing, human review, logging and deployment safety checks are complete.
