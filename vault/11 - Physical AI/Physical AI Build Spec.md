## Common platform patterns

- **Pi 5 voice shell:** Raspberry Pi 5 + Whisplay HAT + ReSpeaker mic + I2S speaker bonnet + M.2 NVMe, in a 3D-printed Bambu or Prusa shell.
- **Jetson inference node:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super paired with a Pi 5 UI controller and camera (64MP Hawkeye for document scanning, AI Camera or OAK-D Lite for perception).
- **BLE wearable:** Feather nRF52840 Sense / Nano 33 BLE Sense / XIAO ESP32S3 Sense + DRV2605L haptics + PowerBoost 1000 LiPo, TPU/PETG printed shell.
- **Mesh:** LILYGO T-Beam / T-Deck and Heltec V3 Meshtastic nodes with a Pi 5 MQTT bridge.
- **Mobile autonomy:** Holybro X500 V2 + Pixhawk 6C drones and ROSMASTER R2 rovers, Jetson companion, e-stop and fused rails, under Aegis-Hold.
- **Mac mini division nodes:** M4 / M4 Pro Mac minis per division, with Synology NAS shares and labeled Cat6A VLAN drops.
