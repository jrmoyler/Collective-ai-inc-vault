# Wearable Catalog — Operating Standard

Cover statement and operating standard of the [[Wearable Catalog]].

## Cover
| Item | Statement |
|---|---|
| Scope | Parent company + 20 divisions. Five distinct hardware products per entity. 105 catalog entries total. |

| Safety gate | Physical autonomy remains staged under Aegis-Hold until testing, human review, logging, and deployment safety checks are complete. |

The document is labeled a private operating blueprint and prototype catalog generated for [[JR Moyler]] / [[HATAALII]].

## Operating standard
- The catalog goes beyond badges and wrist monitors into rings, pendants, ear cues, garment modules, shoe/boot pods, smart gloves, body patches, shoulder clips, lanyards, key tokens, and pocket control objects.
- Every entry is an auditable physical AI endpoint: a body-worn form factor, a power profile, a BLE/NFC/mesh identity path, a data workflow, and an Aegis safety rule.
- Product concepts avoid duplicate names and duplicate product roles across the parent company and the 20 divisions. Shared electronics are reused only as standardized internal platforms ([[Wearable Catalog — Shared Platforms]]).
- Clinical, legal, civic, financial, behavioral, security, and robotics-adjacent products are scoped as prototype/research/control devices unless explicitly cleared by the relevant review workflow.

> [!warning] Health-related wearables
> 21 entries touch health, wellness, clinical or behavioral data. Each carries a clinical-oversight callout and must clear Aegis review before production. None is a medical device; safety gates forbid diagnosis or treatment claims.
