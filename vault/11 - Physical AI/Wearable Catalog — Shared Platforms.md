## Core platform roles (from the hardware stacks)
- **Feather nRF52840 Sense**: smart rings and some fobs. BLE identity, tap/haptic control.
- **XIAO ESP32S3 Sense**: pendants, ear cues, camera/mic shoulder and lens clips. Voice snippets, POV stills.
- **Circuit Playground Bluefruit**: lanyards, key fobs, pocket tokens and pendants. Buttons, RGB, NFC pairing.
- **Nano 33 BLE Sense**: patches, belt/shoe/ankle pods, palm straps, sleeves. IMU motion and posture proxies.
- **DRV2605L**: haptic driver used across most entries.
