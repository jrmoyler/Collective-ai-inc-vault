# Wearable Catalog

Body-worn physical AI catalog for the parent company and the 20 divisions: 5 non-repeating devices per entity, **105 entries**. All are prototype concepts staged under Aegis-Hold. Only 9 divisions are operating; devices for the 11 chartered divisions are concepts tied to chartered units.

## Totals
| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Entries | 105 |
| Entities | 21 (parent + 20 divisions) |
| Sum of low budgets (one of each) | $10,890 |
| Sum of high budgets (one of each) | $17,910 |
| Lowest entry budget | $65 (Volunteer Key Token) |
| Highest entry budget | $280 (Prime Gesture Glove) |
| Smart rings | 19 |
| Health-related entries (clinical-oversight callout) | 21 |

Budget sums are computed from the per-entry ranges; the catalog does not state a total.

> [!warning] Clinical oversight
> Health-related wearables are research/wellness devices only. Each needs clinical review for any health claim, keeps health data local/encrypted, and needs Aegis review before production. See [[Wearable Catalog — Build Governance]].
